# ☕ Doodh Wali Masala Chai

> **The Secret Concept:**
> Traditional chai recipes often boil strong black tea leaves in plain water first, which can draw out harsh tannins and
> make the tea bitter if left too long. This technique uses whole milk heated before or during infusion, allowing the fats
> in milk to bind with the essential oils in spices and tea leaves. The result is a silky, rich, perfectly aromatic cup
> without any astringent bitterness.

---

## 📊 Overview

* **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
* **Cook Time:** 8–10 minutes
* **Servings:** 2 cups
* **Cuisine:** Indian

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### **Base Liquids & Essentials:**

* **Full-Cream / Whole Milk:** $1\frac{1}{2}$ cups
* **Water:** $\frac{1}{2}$ cup *(just enough to gently steam/start the ginger)*
* **CTC Black Tea Leaves:** $1\frac{1}{2}$ to $2$ tbsp (e.g., Red Label, Wagh Bakri, or Society tea)
* **Sugar or Rock Sugar (Mishri):** $2$ to $3$ tsp (adjust to taste)

### **Fresh & Whole Aromatics:**

* **Fresh Ginger:** 1-inch piece (lightly peeled and coarsely crushed in a mortar & pestle)
* **Green Cardamom Pods:** 3 pods (cracked open to expose seeds)
* **Cloves:** 2 whole
* **Black Peppercorns:** 2 to 3 whole berries
* **Cinnamon Stick:** $\frac{1}{2}$-inch stick (optional)
* **Chai Masala Powder:** $\frac{1}{4}$ tsp (optional, added at the very end)

---

## 🍳 Instructions

### **1. Extract the Fresh Ginger Base**

1. In a small chai pot (saucepan), add the **$\frac{1}{2}$ cup water** and the freshly crushed **ginger**.
2. Bring to a short simmer over medium heat for **60 seconds** to release the fresh juice and spice from the ginger
   without boiling down the liquid.

### **2. Incorporate the Milk & Whole Aromatics**

1. Pour in the **$1\frac{1}{2}$ cups of full-cream milk**.
2. Add the cracked **green cardamom pods**, whole **cloves**, **black peppercorns**, and **cinnamon stick** directly
   into the cold/warm milk mixture.
3. Bring the milk mixture to a gentle, steady warm simmer over low-medium heat.

### **3. Add Tea Leaves & Steep**

1. Once the milk is hot and barely simmering, stir in the **CTC black tea leaves** and **sugar**.
2. Lower the heat to low. Allow the chai to gently steep and roll over low heat for **3 to 4 minutes**.
3. Watch the color transform into a rich, deep caramel shade. Gently stir or lift and pour back with a ladle twice to
   aerate the milk tea.

### **4. Final Touch & Strain**

1. *Optional:* Sprinkle $\frac{1}{4}$ tsp of ground **chai masala powder** in the final 30 seconds to lock in fresh
   top-note aromas.
2. Turn off the flame immediately once the single final boil rises.
3. Strain through a fine mesh sieve directly into heated cups or a kulhad (clay cup). Serve hot alongside biscuits or
   samosas!

## 💡 Golden Rules & Pro Tips

| Mistake                             | What Happens                                     | Asha's Fix                                          |
|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **Boiling tea vigorously in water** | Extracts harsh, astringent tannins               | Steep tea leaves gently in milk base                |
| **Adding cold milk to boiling tea** | Drops brewing temp abruptly, causing thin flavor | Add warm/hot milk or heat milk directly with spices |
| **Steeping past 4 minutes**         | Tea turns bitter and harsh                       | Keep infusion time to 3–4 minutes max               |

> 📌 **Golden Quote from the Video:**
> *"If the cup comes out bitter, the tea went too long; if it tastes thin, the milk went in too fast."*
