# Quick and Healthy Sambar for an Octogenarian

This is a mild, soft, low-oil South Indian sambar that is easier to chew and digest. It takes about **25 minutes**.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup toor dal
- 2 to 2 1/2 cups water, as needed
- 1 small tomato, chopped
- 1/2 to 1 cup soft vegetables, such as pumpkin, carrot, bottle gourd, zucchini, or squash
- 1 to 1 1/2 teaspoons sambar powder
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 to 2 teaspoons tamarind paste, or a small piece of tamarind soaked in warm water
- A modest amount of salt
- 1/2 teaspoon jaggery (optional)

## For Tempering

- 1 teaspoon oil
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
- A pinch of hing
- 5 to 6 curry leaves

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Wash the dal.** Rinse the toor dal two or three times.

2. **Fill the cooker.** Add the dal, turmeric, tomato, vegetables, and 1 1/2 cups water to a pressure cooker or Instant Pot.

3. **Cook until very soft.**
   - **Pressure cooker:** Cook for 3 to 4 whistles.
   - **Instant Pot:** Cook on High Pressure for 8 minutes, then allow a 10-minute natural pressure release.

4. **Mash well.** Open the cooker and mash the dal and vegetables. Add 1/2 to 1 cup hot water so the sambar is slightly thinner than usual.

5. **Add flavoring.** Stir in the sambar powder, tamarind, and a modest amount of salt. Add jaggery if the tamarind tastes too sharp.

6. **Simmer.** Simmer uncovered for 5 to 7 minutes. Add more hot water if it becomes too thick. The vegetables should be soft enough to mash with a spoon.

7. **Prepare the tempering.** Heat 1 teaspoon oil in a small pan. Add mustard seeds. Once they pop, add cumin seeds, hing, and curry leaves. Cook for about 20 seconds.

8. **Finish the sambar.** Pour the tempering into the sambar and simmer for 1 more minute. Taste and adjust the water or salt if needed.

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve warm with very soft rice or idli.
- Keep the sambar mild, fairly thin, low in oil, and moderately salted.
- If chewing is difficult, mash the vegetables completely before serving.
- Avoid very spicy sambar powder and large pieces of drumstick if swallowing or chewing is difficult.

> **Health note:** If the person has kidney disease, diabetes, swallowing difficulty, or a prescribed low-sodium diet, adjust the ingredients according to their clinician's advice.
