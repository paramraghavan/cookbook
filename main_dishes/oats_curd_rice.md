# South Indian Oats Curd Rice (Oats Thayir Sadam)

Oats curd rice is a healthy, creamy, and quick alternative to regular rice or
millet curd rice.

It is especially suitable for elderly people because of its soft texture, ease
of chewing, and simple preparation.

Oats contain **beta-glucan**, a soluble fiber that can help reduce LDL
cholesterol.

- **Preparation and Cooking Time:** 10–15 minutes
- **Servings:** 2
- **Diet:** Vegetarian

---

## 1. Ingredients

| Ingredient                  | Quantity  |
|-----------------------------|-----------|
| Rolled oats (old-fashioned) | ½ cup     |
| Water                       | 1–1¼ cups |
| Fresh plain curd (yogurt)   | ¾ cup     |
| Milk (optional)             | ¼ cup     |
| Grated ginger               | ½ tsp     |
| Grated carrot               | 2 tbsp    |
| Salt                        | To taste  |

### For Tempering

| Ingredient             | Quantity |
|------------------------|----------|
| Ghee                   | 1 tsp    |
| Mustard seeds          | ½ tsp    |
| Curry leaves           | 5–6      |
| Hing (asafoetida)      | A pinch  |
| Urad dal (optional)    | ½ tsp    |
| Green chili (optional) | ½        |

---

## 2. Preparation

### Step 1: Cook the Oats

1. Add ½ cup rolled oats to a saucepan.
2. Pour in 1–1¼ cups water.
3. Cook over low-medium heat for 5–7 minutes.
4. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.
5. Continue cooking until the oats become soft and creamy.

**Tip:** For elderly people, use 1¼ cups water and cook until the oats are very
soft.

### Step 2: Cool the Oats

1. Remove the saucepan from the heat.
2. Allow the cooked oats to cool until lukewarm.
3. Stir occasionally to prevent lumps.

**Important:** Oats absorb additional water as they cool, so the mixture may
become thicker.

### Step 3: Mix with Curd

1. Add ¾ cup fresh curd to the cooled oats.
2. Add ¼ cup milk if desired.
3. Mix in:
    - Grated ginger
    - Grated carrot
    - Salt
4. Stir until smooth and creamy.
5. Add a little water if the consistency is too thick.

**Tip:** Avoid adding curd when the oats are very hot.

### Step 4: Prepare Tempering

1. Heat 1 tsp ghee in a small pan.
2. Add mustard seeds and allow them to splutter.
3. Add optional urad dal and sauté briefly.
4. Add curry leaves, hing, and optional green chili.
5. Fry briefly until aromatic.

### Step 5: Combine and Serve

1. Pour the tempering over the oats-curd mixture.
2. Mix thoroughly.
3. Adjust the consistency if necessary.
4. Serve fresh.

## 4. Tips for Elderly People (85+)

For elderly people who prefer soft, easy-to-digest meals:

- Use **rolled oats** instead of steel-cut oats.
- Cook oats with extra water until very soft.
- Use fresh, non-sour curd.
- Avoid green chili if sensitive to spicy foods.
- Skip whole urad dal if chewing is difficult.
- Add a little grated ginger for flavor.
- Use 1 tsp ghee for mild tempering.
- Keep the final consistency soft and creamy.

### Additional Protein

To increase protein content:

1. Replace some or all of the regular curd with plain Greek yogurt or strained
   curd.
2. Add a little water to maintain a creamy consistency.
3. Mix thoroughly before serving.

**Note:** The protein content depends on the type and quantity of yogurt used.

---

## 5. Preparing Oats Curd Rice Ahead for Lunch

Oats curd rice can be prepared in the morning, but it requires proper
refrigeration.

### Recommended Method

1. Cook oats until very soft.
2. Allow them to cool briefly.
3. Refrigerate the cooked oats promptly.
4. Store the curd separately in the refrigerator when practical.
5. Combine the oats, curd, and tempering closer to serving time.

### Preventing Thickening

Oats naturally absorb additional liquid over time.

To maintain a creamy consistency:

- Cook oats with slightly more water.
- Add a little cold water or milk before serving.
- Stir thoroughly to restore the creamy texture.

### Food Safety

- Store oats curd rice at 40°F (4°C) or below.
- Do not leave it at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
- If the temperature exceeds 90°F (32°C), limit unrefrigerated time to 1 hour.
- Avoid keeping curd rice in a heated lunch box for several hours.

## 6. Recommended Version for Elderly People

### Soft and Creamy Oats Curd Rice

#### Ingredients

- ½ cup rolled oats
- 1¼ cups water
- ¾ cup fresh curd
- ¼ cup milk (optional)
- ½ tsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp ghee
- ½ tsp mustard seeds
- 4–5 curry leaves
- Pinch of hing
- Salt to taste

#### Method

1. Cook oats with 1¼ cups water for 5–7 minutes until very soft.
2. Stir and mash slightly if needed.
3. Allow the oats to cool until lukewarm.
4. Add curd, optional milk, ginger, and salt.
5. Mix until smooth and creamy.
6. Heat ghee and temper mustard seeds, curry leaves, and hing.
7. Add the tempering to the oats mixture.
8. Adjust consistency with a little water if necessary.
9. Serve fresh.

**Result:** Soft, creamy South Indian oats curd rice with a mild flavor and
smooth texture.

## 7. Serving Suggestions

Oats curd rice pairs well with:

- Mild cucumber pachadi
- Soft cooked carrots
- Beetroot poriyal
- Lightly sautéed beans
- Small quantity of mango pickle (optional)

For elderly people, soft cooked vegetables are preferable to crunchy
accompaniments.

## 8. Nutritional Benefits

### Oats

- Contain soluble fiber called beta-glucan.
- Can support healthy cholesterol levels.
- Provide complex carbohydrates.
- Supply some plant-based protein.
- Help promote fullness.

### Curd (Yogurt)

- Provides protein.
- Supplies calcium.
- May contain beneficial live cultures.
- Adds creaminess and flavor.

### Ginger

- Adds mild flavor.
- May help relieve mild nausea or digestive discomfort in some people.

### Ghee

- Enhances flavor.
- Adds fat and calories.
- Best used in moderate quantities.

## 9. Final Recommendation

For a quick and nourishing South Indian meal, **Rolled Oats Curd Rice** is an
excellent choice.

### Preferred Combination

- Rolled oats
- Fresh, non-sour curd
- Grated ginger
- Small amount of ghee
- Mustard seeds
- Curry leaves
- Soft grated carrot

### Why Choose Oats Curd Rice?

1. Faster to cook than millet or regular rice.
2. Naturally soft and creamy.
3. Provides soluble fiber.
4. Easy to customize for elderly people.
5. Requires very little oil.
6. Suitable for a light lunch or dinner.

### Oats vs Samai: Which Is Better?

**Choose oats curd rice if:**

- You want a quick 10–15-minute meal.
- A very soft, creamy texture is preferred.
- You want to increase soluble fiber intake.

**Choose samai millet curd rice if:**

- You prefer a texture closer to traditional rice.
- You enjoy the mild, grain-like texture of millet.
- You want variety in your whole-grain meals.

**Overall Recommendation:**

For an elderly person who prefers soft meals, rolled oats curd rice is a
convenient and nutritious option.

For variety, alternate between oats curd rice, samai millet curd rice, and
traditional rice-based curd rice.
