# Oats Upma — Option A (Frozen Bell Pepper and Onion)

A quick, vegetarian, egg-free breakfast using **rolled oats** and frozen bell pepper and onion. No need to thaw the vegetables first.

**Servings:** 2  
**Preparation:** 5 minutes  
**Cooking:** 12–15 minutes

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rolled oats (old-fashioned oats) | 1 cup |
| Frozen diced bell pepper and onion mix | 1 cup |
| Oil | 2 tsp |
| Mustard seeds | 1/2 tsp |
| Cumin seeds (jeera) | 1/2 tsp |
| Urad dal (optional) | 1 tsp |
| Curry leaves (optional) | 6–8 |
| Ginger, grated | 1 tsp |
| Green chili, finely chopped (optional) | 1 |
| Turmeric | 1/4 tsp |
| Salt | About 1/2 tsp, adjust to taste |
| Water | 3/4 cup to start; add more as needed |
| Lemon juice | 1–2 tsp |
| Fresh cilantro (optional) | For garnish |

## Step-by-step instructions

1. **Dry-roast the oats.** Heat a pan over medium-low heat. Add 1 cup rolled oats and stir for **2–3 minutes**, until lightly toasted and fragrant. Transfer to a bowl. Do not brown them heavily.
2. **Prepare the tempering.** In the same pan, warm 2 tsp oil over medium heat. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter. Add cumin seeds and urad dal (if using). Stir until the dal turns lightly golden.
3. **Add aromatics.** Add curry leaves, grated ginger, and chopped green chili, if using. Stir for about **20–30 seconds**.
4. **Cook frozen vegetables.** Add **1 cup frozen bell pepper and onion directly from the freezer**. Sauté uncovered for **4–6 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the water released from the vegetables mostly evaporates and the onion softens.
5. **Season.** Add turmeric and salt; mix for 15–20 seconds.
6. **Add water.** Pour in **3/4 cup water** and bring to a gentle simmer. The frozen vegetables may have released some moisture, so start with less water rather than too much.
7. **Mix in oats.** Reduce heat to low. Add the toasted oats, stir well, and cook for **2–3 minutes** until softened but not mushy. If the mixture looks dry before the oats are done, add water **1–2 tbsp at a time**.
8. **Rest.** Turn off the heat, cover, and let the upma sit for **2 minutes** so the oats absorb remaining moisture.
9. **Finish and serve.** Fluff gently. Add lemon juice and cilantro, if desired. Serve warm.

## Tips

- **For fluffy upma:** Use old-fashioned rolled oats, roast first, and avoid excess water.
- **For softer upma:** Add an extra 1/4 cup water in small increments and cook a little longer.
- **For frozen vegetables:** Do not thaw; cook uncovered so excess water can evaporate.
- **For quick oats:** They cook faster and become softer; reduce added water and cook only briefly.
- **Optional additions:** Green peas, grated carrot, or roasted peanuts can be added for variety.
