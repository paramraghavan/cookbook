# For 1 cup Sona Masuri rice:

* Wash the rice 2–3 times.
* Soak it for 20–30 minutes.
* Cook it with 3 cups water. If she needs very soft rice, use 3¼ cups.
* Pressure-cook for 4 whistles, then allow the pressure to release naturally.
* While the rice is still hot, gently mash it.
* Add ¼ cup boiling water and mix. It should be slightly wetter than normally served.
* Optionally mix in ½ teaspoon ghee if her diet permits.
* Immediately transfer the hot rice to a flat-bottomed glass or metal container with a tightly sealed lid. Leave a
  little empty space at the top.
* For additional moisture, sprinkle another 2 tablespoons of boiling water over the rice before closing the container.
  Do not add cold water.
* Keep the container directly on the HOTLOGIC heating plate
