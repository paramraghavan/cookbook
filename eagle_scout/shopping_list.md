## 📝 SHOPPING CHECKLIST

### Before You Go
- [ ] Print this list or save to phone
- [ ] Check Home Depot website for item availability
- [ ] Look for 10% off coupons
- [ ] Check for bulk discounts on screws/nails

### At Home Depot
- [ ] Go to **Lumber & Plywood** section first
- [ ] Pick up **3 sheets of 3/4" plywood** (may need cart help)
- [ ] Grab **2x 1×2" pine** and **1x 2×4" pine** boards
- [ ] Head to **Hardware** for fasteners, clamps, bits
- [ ] Get **Titebond II glue** in **Paint aisle**
- [ ] Pick up **sandpaper, filler, primer, paint** in **Paint section**
- [ ] Consider **paint tools** (roller, brushes) if needed

### After Purchase
- [ ] Have materials delivered or rent a cart to load vehicle
- [ ] Check receipt against this list
- [ ] Keep receipt for 30-day returns
- [ ] Store materials in dry location before assembly

## 🛠️ RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS (HOME DEPOT)

### Fasteners
- **Screws:** Grip-Rite or Spax Star-Drive screws
- **Nails:** Hitachi or Senco finish/brad nails
- **Glue:** Titebond II (yellow glue) — Essential

### Tools
- **Countersink:** Irwin or DeWalt 82° adjustable ($10-15)
- **Clamps:** Bessey or Irwin bar/spring clamps
- **Sandpaper:** 3M or generic multi-grit packs

### Finishing
- **Primer:** Kilz or Minwax latex primer
- **Paint:** Sherwin-Williams Duration or Benjamin Moore Advance (Semi-gloss)
- **Polyurethane:** Minwax or Varathane water-based

## 💡 PRO TIPS

✅ **Bulk Discounts:** Ask about volume pricing on screws/nails
✅ **Coupons:** Check email for 10-20% off coupons
✅ **Delivery:** Free delivery on orders over $45 (check eligibility)
✅ **Rental Tools:** Home Depot rents circular saws, sanders, etc. if needed
✅ **Lumber Quality:** Inspect boards for warping/defects before checkout
✅ **Returns:** Keep receipt — 30-day return policy on most items
✅ **Pro Advice:** Talk to Home Depot staff in lumber section for cuts

## 📞 QUESTIONS TO ASK AT HOME DEPOT

- "Do you have BCX plywood in stock?" (best for cabinets)
- "Can you suggest a good semi-gloss enamel paint for a lab cabinet?"
- "Are there any sales on wood screws this week?"
- "Do you carry Titebond II?"
- "Can you cut the plywood sheets?" (some locations offer cuts)
