# Chemistry Lab Floor Storage Shelf (Shelf A)

## Complete Step-by-Step DIY Build & Material Guide

---

### Key Specifications

* **Overall Dimensions:** 92" Wide × 23.25" Deep × 25.5" High (24" Body + 1.5" Base Skids)
* **Design Features:** Easy cleat assembly, anti-sag long shelf reinforcement, base elevation skids against damp floors,
  and chemical-resistant finishing for lab use.

---

### 1. Shopping List (Home Depot / Lowe's)

#### Lumber & Plywood

* **3 Sheets — 3/4" × 4' × 8' Sanded Plywood:** Select **BCX** or **Sandeply Birch**. (Used for frame, back panel, and
  shelves).
* **2 Boards — 1" × 2" × 8' Select Pine:** One board for the Long Shelf stiffener lip; one board for wall cleats.
* **1 Board — 2" × 4" × 8' Construction Pine:** Used for base skids/feet underneath the unit to elevate it off damp lab
  floors.

> **Pro Scout Tip:** Ask the store associate at the Home Depot or Lowe's lumber panel saw to rip your 4' × 8' sheets
> into the 23.25" and 24.0" wide strips for free or cheap. This makes transporting the wood much easier and guarantees
> straight cuts!

#### Fasteners, Hardware & Glue

* **1 Bottle — Titebond II Wood Glue (16 oz):** Waterproof/water-resistant glue (provides 80%+ structural strength).
* **1 Box — 2" #8 Star-Drive Cabinet/Wood Screws:** Main assembly screws for joining outer frame and shelves.
* **1 Box — 1-1/4" #8 Star-Drive Wood Screws:** For attaching the back panel, stiffener lip, and wall cleats.
* **1 Bit — 1/8" Countersink Drill Bit Combination:** Pre-drills pilot holes and carves a small pocket so screw heads
  sit flush with the wood without splitting it.

#### Lab-Grade Paint & Finishing

* **1 Quart — Latex Wood Primer:** Seals raw plywood edges so they don't soak up paint.
* **1 Gallon — Semi-Gloss or Gloss Interior Enamel Paint:** Creates a smooth, washable finish that resists water and
  common chemical splashes.
* **1 Quart — Water-Based Polyurethane (Clear):** Adds a tough protective clear coat over horizontal shelf surfaces for
  maximum scratch and stain resistance.
* **1 Pack — 120-Grit Sandpaper + 4" Foam Rollers & Brushes**

### 5. Step-by-Step Build Instructions

#### Step 1: Prepare Long Shelf Stiffener Lip

1. Take your $60.25''$ Long Shelf and lay it flat on your workbench bottom-side up.
2. Cut a $60.25''$ piece from your 1x2 pine board. Stand it on its narrow edge flush against the underside front edge of
   the shelf to form an upside-down "L" shape.
3. Apply wood glue between the pieces. Pre-drill pilot holes using your 1/8" countersink bit and drive 1-1/4" screws
   straight down through the shelf into the stiffener board every 8 inches.

#### Step 2: Attach Base Skids to Bottom Panel

1. Lay the **Bottom Panel** ($92'' \times 23.25''$) flat on the floor face down.
2. Take your three $23.25''$ long 2x4 pieces (Base Skids). Place one at the far left edge, one in the exact middle, and
   one at the far right edge (running from front to back).
3. Glue, pre-drill with your countersink bit, and drive 2" screws through the bottom panel into the 2x4 skids (4 screws
   per skid). This elevates the cabinet 1.5" off floor moisture.

#### Step 3: Mount Wall Cleats (The Easy Assembly Method)

1. Measure from the bottom edge of your vertical wall panels and draw horizontal guidelines:

* **Left Side Panel & Left Face of Center Divider:** Draw lines at **$5.5''$**, **$11.0''$**, and **$16.5''$** from the
  bottom.
* **Right Side Panel & Right Face of Center Divider:** Draw a line at **$14.5''$** from the bottom.


2. Position a $20''$ long 1x2 cleat *just below each line*, pushing the cleat flush against the rear edge (leaving
   a $3.25''$ space at the front).
3. Apply wood glue behind each cleat, pre-drill 3 pilot holes per cleat, and secure with 1-1/4" screws.

#### Step 4: Assemble Interior Shelves & Frame

1. Stand the **Left Side Panel**, **Center Divider**, and **Right Side Panel** vertically.
2. Slide the 3 Small Shelves ($29.5''$) and 1 Long Shelf ($60.25''$) into position so they rest flat on top of the
   mounted wall cleats.
3. Apply wood glue along all contact points.
4. Drive 2" screws from the outer wall faces into the shelf ends, and drive 1-1/4" screws straight down through the top
   of the shelves into the cleats below.

#### Step 5: Attach Top, Bottom & Back Panels

1. Apply wood glue to all top edges of the assembled vertical walls.
2. Place the **Top Panel** ($92'' \times 23.25''$) squarely on top, pre-drill, and secure with 2" screws into the
   vertical panel edges (3 screws per wall).
3. Apply glue to all bottom edges, set the frame onto the **Bottom Panel** (with attached skids), and secure with 2"
   screws driven from inside down into the bottom panel.
4. Lay the entire cabinet face down. Run glue around the full rear perimeter and center divider edge.
5. Set the **Back Panel** ($92'' \times 24''$) flush over the back. Pre-drill and drive 1-1/4" screws every 6 to 8
   inches around the perimeter and into the center divider. *This back panel locks the structure square and prevents
   side-to-side racking.*

#### Step 6: Sanding & Lab Chemical-Proof Finishing

1. Sand all outer corners, exposed edges, and screw locations with 120-grit sandpaper until completely smooth to prevent
   splinters.
2. Wipe away all wood dust thoroughly with a damp rag.
3. Apply **1 coat of Latex Interior Wood Primer** to all surfaces and let dry for 2 hours.
4. Apply **2 coats of Semi-Gloss or Gloss Interior Enamel Paint**, allowing full dry time between coats. Gloss enamel
   provides a hard, non-porous surface that wipes clean easily.
5. Apply **1 coat of Clear Water-Based Polyurethane** over the top table surface and shelf tops to lock out chemical
   spills, water, and scratches.
