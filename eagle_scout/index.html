🔄 Recommended Workflow

For First-Time Builders:

  1. Start with the Builder Assistant - answer a few questions about your setup
  2. Review the Assembly Guide to understand the overall structure
  3. Download and print the Materials to Buy PDF
  4. Review the Fastener Guide to understand nails vs screws
  5. Keep the Assembly Checklist nearby during building
  6. Reference the 3D Visualization when assembling parts

For Custom Dimensions:

  1. Use the Flexible Builder to customize width, depth, height, and shelves
  2. Preview your design in 2D (front and side views) and 3D
  3. Download cutting list for your custom dimensions
  4. Use the generated specifications to purchase materials
  5. Follow the main Assembly Guide adapted to your configuration

For Testing Design:

  1. Build a 10% Scaled Prototype to test your design (optional but recommended)
  2. Verify fit, stability, and shelf spacing with the prototype
  3. Make any necessary adjustments
  4. Proceed with full-size build using standard dimensions or Flexible Builder

📌 Key Information

Standard Dimensions (Default Configuration)

Overall Size: 92" W × 23.25" D × 24" H
Material: 3/4" Plywood + Pine lumber support structure
Weight: Approximately 150-180 lbs when fully assembled
Build Time: 6-8 hours (plus drying time)

Main Parts (P1-P11)

The shelf is constructed from 11 main parts groups:

  • P1-P2: Top and bottom panels (exterior)
  • P3-P6: Frame panels - back, left side, right side, center divider
  • P7-P8: Shelves - long shelf (right) and small shelves (left, 3 pieces)
  • P9-P11: Support structure - stiffener, cleats, and base skids

Materials Overview

  • Plywood: 3 sheets of 3/4" plywood
  • Pine Lumber: 1×2 boards (cleats and stiffener) and 2×4 boards (base skids)
  • Fasteners: Wood screws (1.5" and 2.5"), finish nails, brad nails
  • Adhesive: Titebond II wood glue (1 bottle)
  • Hardware: C-clamps and bar clamps for assembly

Assembly Sequence

The critical assembly sequence is:

  1. Attach P9 (stiffener) to P7 (long shelf)
  2. Attach P11 (base skids) to P2 (bottom panel)
  3. Install P10 (cleats) on vertical panels P4, P5, P6
  4. Attach shelves P7 and P8 to cleats
  5. Assemble frame with glue and screws
  6. Square the frame using P3 (back panel) - critical step!
  7. Attach P1 (top panel) and finalize

💡 Pro Tips

Before You Start

  • Print the Assembly Guide and Cutting Diagram before going to the lumberyard
  • Read through the entire Assembly Guide once before starting
  • Gather and verify all tools and materials on the checklist
  • Clear plenty of workspace - you'll need it!

During Assembly

  • Use the 3D Visualization while assembling - it's helpful reference
  • Pay special attention to diagonal measurements when squaring (P3 attachment)
  • Allow proper curing time for glue - don't rush this step
  • Check cleats are level before screwing shelves into place
  • Use countersinking for wood screws for a professional finish

After Assembly

  • Allow 24-48 hours for full glue cure before applying heavy loads
  • Apply finish (stain/paint) if desired for durability
  • Use the base skids to elevate the shelf for moisture protection
  • Load evenly to distribute weight across all shelves
