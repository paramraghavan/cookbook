QUICK BUILD STEPS (20-30 Minutes)

STEP 1:
Gather & Cut Cardboard
Cut small cardboard pieces using dimensions from table above. Use a utility knife or scissors. Mark each piece with its Part ID (P1-P11).
STEP 2:
Assemble Vertical Frame
Glue P4 (left), P5 (right), and P6 (center) vertically together. These are your main support walls. Let dry 5 minutes.
STEP 3:
Add Support Cleats (P10)
Glue small cardboard strips (P10 cleats) horizontally on the inside faces at shelf heights. Total of 8 small cleats. Let dry 5 minutes.
STEP 4:
Position Shelves
Glue small shelf pieces (P8) and long shelf (P7) on top of the cleats. This shows your shelf layout. Let dry 5 minutes.
STEP 5:
Attach Top, Bottom, Back
Glue P1 (top) and P2 (bottom) panels to the frame. Glue P3 (back panel) to lock everything square. Use tape to reinforce joints.
STEP 6:
Add Base Skids (Optional)
Glue three small P11 skids under the bottom panel. This shows elevation concept. Let dry 5 minutes.
Total Time: ~25 minutes (most time is glue drying)

WHAT TO LOOK AT

  • ✓ Overall Proportions: Does the shape/ratio look right?
  • ✓ Shelf Layout: Are 3 small + 1 long shelf arrangement good?
  • ✓ Height Distribution: Is the spacing between shelves appropriate?
  • ✓ Back Panel Effect: Does back panel lock it square?
  • ✓ Visual Concept: Does design look good overall?
  • ✓ Reference Model: Good desk decoration or design reference
Note: This is NOT for testing load capacity or assembly techniques. It's purely a visual reference to see if the design looks right before building full-size.
