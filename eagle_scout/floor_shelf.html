Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions

1
Prepare Long Shelf Stiffener Lip [P9]
Parts Used:
[P7] Long Shelf (60.25" × 23.25")
[P9] Shelf Stiffener Lip (60.25" × 1.5")

Instructions:

  1. Lay the P7 (Long Shelf) flat on your workbench with the BOTTOM side facing UP.
  2. Position P9 (the 1×2 board) along the underside of P7, with the front edge of P9 flush against the front edge of P7 (creating an inverted "L" beam).
  3. Apply wood glue along the ENTIRE contact joint (bottom of P7 where it meets top of P9).
  4. Clamp firmly in place to hold alignment.
Glue Application: Apply 1/4" wide continuous bead along the contact surface. Wipe squeeze-out with damp cloth within 15 minutes.
Fastening Method:
  • Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" countersink bit through TOP surface of P7 (the visible side after assembly)
  • Space holes every 6-8 inches along the 60.25" length (approximately 8-9 nails)
  • Drive 1.25"-1.5" finish nails or wood screws VERTICALLY DOWN through P7 into P9
  • Ensure nails are driven fully flush (countersink screws 1/16" below surface)

Wait 30 minutes before moving assembly.

Figure A: P9 stiffener lip mounted under front edge of P7 (creates inverted L-beam)
2
Attach 2×4 Base Skids [P11] to Bottom Panel [P2]
Parts Used:
[P2] Bottom Panel (92" × 23.25")
[P11] Base Skids × 3 (23.25" × 3.5" each)

Instructions:

  1. Lay P2 (Bottom Panel) flat on the floor with the BOTTOM side facing DOWN (the side that will be against the floor).
  2. Position the three P11 skids on top (the visible surface):
    • Left Skid: Flush with the far left edge (running front-to-back)
    • Center Skid: Positioned exactly in the middle (at 46" from left edge)
    • Right Skid: Flush with the far right edge (running front-to-back)
  3. Ensure all skids are parallel and perpendicular to the front edge of P2.
Glue Application: Apply Titebond II to the TOP surface of each skid (the surface touching P2). Use continuous 1/4" bead. Allow 15 minutes of contact before fastening.
Fastening Method:
  • Use 2" #8 wood screws (4 screws per skid = 12 total)
  • Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" countersink bit through P2 into each skid
  • Screw placement: 2 screws at front end of each skid, 2 at rear end
  • Drive screws VERTICALLY DOWN through P2 into P11
  • Ensure screw heads are countersunk flush or slightly recessed

The skids will elevate P2 approximately 1.5" off the floor, protecting against moisture and chemical spills.

Figure B: P11 base skids (3× 2×4 lumber) positioned left, center, and right under P2 panel
3
Mount Support Cleats [P10] on Vertical Panels
Parts Used:
[P4] Left Side Panel (22.5" × 23.25")
[P5] Right Side Panel (22.5" × 23.25")
[P6] Center Divider (22.5" × 23.25")
[P10] Cleats × 8 (20" × 1.5" each)

Instructions - LEFT SECTION (P4 & left face of P6):

  1. Stand panels P4 and P6 vertically and facing each other.
  2. On the INSIDE FACE of each panel, draw horizontal guidelines at:
    • 5.5" from the bottom edge
    • 11.0" from the bottom edge
    • 16.5" from the bottom edge
  3. Position each 20" P10 cleat just BELOW the marked line, with the cleat's top surface touching or just below the line.
  4. The cleat should be flush against the REAR edge of the panel, leaving a 3.25" gap at the FRONT.

Instructions - RIGHT SECTION (P6 right face & P5):

  1. Stand panels P6 and P5 vertically and facing each other.
  2. On the INSIDE FACE of P5 (and right face of P6), draw ONE horizontal guideline at:
    • 14.5" from the bottom edge
  3. Position the single P10 cleat just BELOW this line.
  4. Flush against the REAR edge, leaving 3.25" gap at FRONT.
Glue Application: Apply Titebond II to the BACK surface of each cleat (the surface touching the panel). Use thin continuous bead. Let cure 5-10 minutes before fastening.
Fastening Method:
  • Use 1.25" finish nails or 1.25" wood screws (3 per cleat)
  • Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" bit
  • Drive nails/screws HORIZONTALLY through the cleat into the panel
  • Placement: Left end, center (10"), and right end of each cleat
  • Ensure cleat is level and flush against panel
4
Position Shelves on Cleats [P7 & P8]
Parts Used:
[P7] Long Shelf (60.25" × 23.25")
[P8] Small Shelves × 3 (29.5" × 23.25" each)
[P10] Cleats (previously mounted)

Instructions:

  1. Carefully slide each shelf horizontally into position, resting on top of the mounting cleats.
  2. Left Section: Position the three P8 small shelves at the three cleat heights (5.5", 11.0", 16.5" from bottom).
  3. Right Section: Position the P7 long shelf at 14.5" from bottom.
  4. Verify that:
    • All shelves are level (use a level tool)
    • Shelves are fully supported along their entire length
    • No gaps between shelf and cleat
Glue Application: Apply Titebond II to the TOP surface of EACH cleat where it contacts the shelf. Use continuous thin bead. Allow 10 minutes before fastening.
Fastening Method - Vertical (Down Through Shelf):
  • Use 1.25"-1.5" finish nails or wood screws
  • Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" countersink bit through TOP surface of shelf
  • Drive nails/screws VERTICALLY DOWN through the shelf into each cleat below
  • Use 3 fasteners per cleat (left end, center, right end)
  • Countersink screw heads 1/16" below surface
Fastening Method - Horizontal (Through Side Panels):
  • Use 1.5"-2" finish nails or 1.625" trim screws
  • Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" bit
  • Drive nails/screws HORIZONTALLY through the side panels (P4, P5) or divider (P6) into the shelf edge
  • Use 2-3 fasteners per shelf side (at shelf ends and center)
  • Aim for center of plywood edge to avoid splitting
Figure D: All shelves (P7 & P8) positioned and secured on mounting cleats (P10)
5
Attach Top & Bottom Panels [P1 & P2]
Parts Used:
[P1] Top Panel (92" × 23.25")
[P2] Bottom Panel with Skids (92" × 23.25")
[P4] [P5] [P6] Vertical panels

Instructions - Attach Top Panel [P1]:

  1. Apply Titebond II to the TOP edges of all three vertical panels (P4, P5, P6).
  2. Carefully place P1 (Top Panel) squarely on top of the frame, ensuring:
    • Front edges are aligned and flush
    • Panel is level (use a level tool)
    • Overhang is equal on all sides
  3. Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" countersink bit through P1 into each vertical panel (3 holes per panel).
  4. Drive 2" #8 wood screws vertically down through P1 into the top edge of each panel.

Instructions - Attach Bottom Panel [P2]:

  1. Apply Titebond II to the BOTTOM edges of all three vertical panels (P4, P5, P6).
  2. Carefully set the frame (now with P1 attached) down onto P2 (which already has P11 skids attached).
  3. Verify:
    • Frame is centered on P2
    • Skids are properly positioned
    • All edges are aligned
  4. Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" countersink bit from INSIDE the cabinet, through each vertical panel edge into P2.
  5. Drive 2" #8 wood screws through panel edges into P2 (3 screws per panel).
Glue Application: Apply Titebond II continuously along the top and bottom edges of all vertical panels. Use approximately 1/4" wide bead. Wipe squeeze-out within 15 minutes.
Figure E: P1 (top) and P2 (bottom) panels attached to vertical frame with all shelves in place
6
Square the Cabinet & Attach Back Panel [P3]
Parts Used:
[P3] Back Panel (92" × 24")
All previously assembled parts

Instructions:

  1. Lay the ENTIRE cabinet assembly face-down on a flat, level surface (or carefully on sawhorses).
  2. Check diagonal measurements from corner to corner:
    • Measure from top-left corner to bottom-right corner
    • Measure from top-right corner to bottom-left corner
    • Both measurements should be exactly equal (within 1/4")
    • If not equal, loosen fasteners and adjust until perfectly square
  3. Apply Titebond II around the ENTIRE rear perimeter and center divider edge (where P3 will attach).
  4. Carefully position P3 (Back Panel) flush over the rear face of the cabinet.
  5. Ensure P3 is flush with all edges and perfectly aligned with the frame.
Fastening Method:
  • Use 1"-1.25" brad nails or wood screws
  • Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" bit around the perimeter
  • Drive fasteners every 6-8 inches around the entire perimeter of P3
  • Also drive fasteners along the center divider (P6) on the back side
  • Use approximately 20-24 fasteners total
  • Ensure all fasteners are driven flush (nails) or countersunk (screws)
Critical Step: The back panel [P3] is the primary component that locks the cabinet rigid and provides structural stability. It MUST be securely fastened. Do not skip this step and do NOT mount this cabinet to walls - the P3 panel provides all needed rigidity for free-standing use.

Allow glue to cure for 2 hours before moving or flipping the cabinet.

Figure F: P3 (back panel) attached to rear - provides structural rigidity and locks cabinet square. NO wall mounting needed.
7
Sand All Surfaces & Edges

Instructions:

  1. Sand all outer corners and exposed edges with 120-grit sandpaper until completely smooth (no splinters).
  2. Pay special attention to:
    • All plywood edges (P1-P8)
    • Top surfaces of shelves (P7, P8)
    • All four outer corners of the cabinet
    • Any visible screw/nail holes
  3. Wipe away all wood dust with a damp rag. Let dry completely (15-30 minutes).
Figure G: Cabinet fully assembled, squared, and ready for primer and paint application

Finishing & Lab Chemical-Proof Painting

8
Prime All Surfaces

Instructions:

  1. Apply 1 coat of Latex Interior Wood Primer to all surfaces (exterior, interior tops of shelves, all edges).
  2. Use foam roller or brush for smooth, even application.
  3. Allow 2 hours drying time before proceeding.
  4. Primer seals the plywood and provides base for enamel paint adhesion.
9
Paint with Chemical-Resistant Enamel

Instructions:

  1. Apply 2 coats of Semi-Gloss or Gloss Interior Enamel Paint to all exterior surfaces.
  2. Allow full drying time between coats (check paint can for specific time - typically 4-6 hours).
  3. Use foam roller or brush for smooth, professional finish.
  4. Paint finish resists water, chemical splashes, and cleaning agents used in chemistry labs.
10
Optional: Protective Polyurethane Top Coat

Instructions:

  1. For maximum protection on shelf tops, apply 1-2 coats of Clear Water-Based Polyurethane.
  2. Focus on:
    • All shelf top surfaces (P7, P8)
    • Top panel (P1) working surface
  3. This provides extra scratch and stain resistance for a chemistry lab environment.
  4. Allow full cure time per manufacturer instructions before loading shelves.

Final Assembly Notes & Safety

Weight Warning: When fully assembled with all plywood and hardware, this cabinet weighs approximately 160 lbs. Always use 3-4 people to lift, move, or flip the cabinet frame. Never attempt alone.

Installation Location

  • Assemble this shelf unit inside or immediately adjacent to the chemistry lab room where it will permanently stay.
  • Place on a level, flat floor.
  • DO NOT WALL MOUNT - This shelf is engineered to be completely free-standing.
  • The internal cleats, back panel, and base skids provide all structural rigidity needed.
  • Ensure floor is clean and level before final placement.

Before Loading Supplies

  • Allow all finishes to fully cure (24-48 hours) before placing any items on shelves.
  • Verify all fasteners are tight and secure.
  • Distribute load evenly across shelves - do not overload single areas.
  • Keep heavier items on lower shelves.

Maintenance

  • Clean shelves regularly with damp cloth and mild soap (suitable for lab environments).
  • Dry immediately to prevent water damage.
  • Check fasteners periodically and tighten if needed.
  • Do not drag or slide on floor - always lift when moving.
