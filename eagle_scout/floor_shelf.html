Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions

1
Prepare Long Shelf Stiffener Lip [P9]
Parts Used:
[P7] Long Shelf (60.25" × 23.25")
[P9] Shelf Stiffener Lip (56" × 1.5") - 2" clearance on each end

Instructions:

  1. Lay the P7 (Long Shelf) flat on your workbench with the BOTTOM side facing UP.
  2. Position P9 (the 1×2 board, 56" long) along the underside of P7, with the front edge of P9 flush against the front edge of P7 (creating an inverted "L" beam).
  3. Centering P9: P9 should be centered under P7, leaving approximately 2" clearance on EACH END. This prevents interference with cleat attachment areas on the vertical panels (P5, P6).
  4. Apply wood glue: Use Titebond II continuous 1/4" bead along entire contact joint (bottom of P7 where it meets top of P9). Glue does 80% of the work.
  5. Clamp: Use 2-3 bar clamps or C-clamps across the joint to hold P9 firmly against P7 bottom while glue sets.
  6. Let glue cure 15 minutes before drilling/fastening.
🔵 GLUE - Titebond II Application:
• Apply continuous 1/4" wide bead along entire contact surface
• Let sit 5-10 minutes for "tack" (surface grab)
• Clamp firmly while curing
• Wipe squeeze-out with damp cloth within 15 minutes
• Full cure: 24 hours (but fasteners can be added after 15 min)
🟡 FASTENERS - Nails vs Screws for P9→P7:
  • Option A - FINISH NAILS (Faster): 1.25"-1.5" finish nails × 8-9
  • Option B - WOOD SCREWS (Stronger): 1.25" #6 wood screws × 8-9 with countersink
→ Recommended: Screws (better structural support for shelf stiffener)
⚙️ INSTALLATION STEPS:
  1. PRE-DRILL: Use 1/8" countersink bit through TOP surface of P7 (spacing 6-8" apart, ~8-9 holes)
  2. IF USING SCREWS: Countersink creates recess for screw head to sit flush
  3. IF USING NAILS: Simply pilot holes to prevent splitting
  4. DRIVE: Fasteners vertically DOWN through P7 into P9 below
  5. FINISH: Ensure nails/screws flush with or slightly below surface (for paint)
⏱️ DRYING TIME - DO NOT SKIP: Wait 30 minutes minimum (clamps on) before moving assembly. Full glue cure: 24 hours, but proceed to next steps after 30 min.
Figure A: P9 stiffener lip mounted under front edge of P7 (creates inverted L-beam)
2
Attach 2×4 Base Skids [P11] to Bottom Panel [P2]
Parts Used:
[P2] Bottom Panel (92" × 23.25")
[P11] Base Skids × 3 (23.25" × 3.5" each)

Instructions:

  1. Lay P2 (Bottom Panel) flat on the floor with the BOTTOM side facing DOWN (the side that will be against the floor).
  2. Position the three P11 skids on top (the visible surface):
    • Left Skid: Flush with the far left edge (running front-to-back)
    • Center Skid: Positioned exactly in the middle (at 46" from left edge)
    • Right Skid: Flush with the far right edge (running front-to-back)
  3. Verify all skids are parallel and perpendicular to the front edge of P2 (use a square).
  4. Apply glue: Use Titebond II continuous 1/4" bead to TOP surface of each skid (the surface touching P2).
  5. Clamp: Use bar clamps or C-clamps to hold skids tight against P2 for 15 minutes before fastening.
🔵 GLUE - Titebond II Application:
• Apply continuous 1/4" bead to TOP surface of each P11 skid
• This is a critical structural joint - carry 100+ lbs weight
• Let glue tack for 15 minutes before driving screws
• Wipe squeeze-out immediately with damp cloth
• Full cure: 24 hours (but 15 min sufficient for fastening)
🟢 FASTENERS - MUST USE WOOD SCREWS for P11→P2 (Structural):
• This is load-bearing: Cabinet + contents = 200+ lbs
• Use: 2" #8 Star-Drive Wood Screws (4 per skid = 12 total)
• DO NOT use nails - insufficient holding power for weight
• Screws provide 2-3x more strength than nails
⚙️ INSTALLATION STEPS - Use Countersink for Flush Finish:
  1. PRE-DRILL: Use 1/8" pilot bit through P2 into P11 (4 holes per skid: 2 front, 2 rear)
  2. COUNTERSINK: Use 82° countersink bit (1/2" diameter for #8 screws) to create recess for screw head
  3. DRIVE: 2" #8 screws vertically DOWN through P2 into P11
  4. FINISH: Screw heads should sit flush or 1/16" below surface (will be primed/painted)
  5. FILL: After paint, fill any visible holes with wood filler if needed
💡 WHY THIS JOINT MATTERS:
P11 skids carry the entire weight of the cabinet and contents (200+ lbs). This is one of the most critical structural connections. Wood screws with countersink and glue provide the necessary strength. This joint must be rock-solid.
⏱️ DRYING TIME: Wait 30 minutes (clamps on) before proceeding. Full cure: 24 hours.

Result: P2 will be elevated approximately 1.5" off the floor, protecting against moisture and chemical spills.

Figure B: P11 base skids (3× 2×4 lumber) positioned left, center, and right under P2 panel
3
Mount Support Cleats [P10] on Vertical Panels
Parts Used:
[P4] Left Side Panel (22.5" × 23.25")
[P5] Right Side Panel (22.5" × 23.25")
[P6] Center Divider (22.5" × 23.25")
[P10] Cleats × 8 (20" × 1.5" each)

Instructions - LEFT SECTION (P4 & left face of P6):

  1. Stand panels P4 and P6 vertically and facing each other (inside surfaces facing inward).
  2. On the INSIDE FACE of each panel, use a measuring tape to mark horizontal guidelines at these heights from the BOTTOM:
    • 5.5" mark - Bottom shelf (P8a) top surface
    • 11.0" mark - Middle shelf (P8b) top surface
    • 16.5" mark - Top shelf (P8c) top surface
  3. Position each 20" P10 cleat with its TOP EDGE aligned with the marked line.
  4. The cleat should be flush against the REAR edge of the panel, leaving a 3.25" gap at the FRONT (where shelf overhang is).

Instructions - RIGHT SECTION (P6 right face & P5):

  1. Stand panels P6 and P5 vertically and facing each other.
  2. On the INSIDE FACE of P5 (and the RIGHT face of P6), draw ONE horizontal guideline at:
    • 14.5" mark - Long shelf (P7) top surface
  3. Position the single P10 cleat with its TOP EDGE at the 14.5" mark.
  4. Flush against the REAR edge, leaving 3.25" gap at FRONT.
🔵 GLUE - Titebond II Application:
• Apply thin continuous 1/4" bead to the BACK surface of each cleat (the surface that touches the panel)
• Position cleat against marked line
• Let glue tack for 5-10 minutes before driving fasteners
• This allows glue to set partially while you drill
🟡 FASTENERS - Nails or Screws for P10→P4, P5, P6:
  • Option A - FINISH NAILS (Faster): 1.25" finish nails × 3 per cleat
  • Option B - WOOD SCREWS (Stronger): 1.25" #6 wood screws × 3 per cleat
→ Recommended: Screws for right section (P7 cleat carries heavier load); nails acceptable for left section
⚙️ INSTALLATION - Horizontal Fastening:
  1. PRE-DRILL: Use 1/8" pilot bit to make 3 holes through cleat into panel (left end, center 10", right end)
  2. DRIVE: 1.25" fasteners HORIZONTALLY through the cleat into the vertical panel
  3. POSITIONING:
    • Left fastener: ~1" from left end of cleat
    • Center fastener: At 10" mark (middle of 20" cleat)
    • Right fastener: ~1" from right end of cleat
  4. ALIGNMENT: Use a level to ensure each cleat is perfectly horizontal before fully tightening
⏱️ DRYING TIME: Wait 10-15 minutes (clamp if possible) after each cleat is fastened before installing shelves. This ensures cleat is solid before shelf load is applied.
4
Position Shelves on Cleats [P7 & P8]
Parts Used:
[P7] Long Shelf (60.25" × 23.25")
[P8] Small Shelves × 3 (29.5" × 23.25" each)
[P10] Cleats (previously mounted)

Instructions:

  1. Carefully slide each shelf horizontally into position, resting on top of the mounting cleats.
  2. Left Section: Position the three P8 small shelves at the three cleat heights (5.5", 11.0", 16.5" from bottom).
  3. Right Section: Position the P7 long shelf at 14.5" from bottom.
  4. Verify that:
    • All shelves are level (use a level tool)
    • Shelves are fully supported along their entire length
    • No gaps between shelf and cleat
🔵 GLUE - Titebond II Application:
• Apply thin continuous 1/4" bead to the TOP surface of EACH cleat (where shelf will rest)
• This primary bonding is critical - shelves must not shift
• Let glue tack for 5-10 minutes before adding fasteners
• Glue does most of the work; fasteners prevent racking
🟡 FASTENERS - Shelves to Cleats (Vertical Fastening):
  • Use: 1.25"-1.5" finish nails or 1.25" wood screws × 3 per cleat
  • Do NOT use structural screws here - vertical load is minimal (glue bears load)
  • Nails are acceptable for small shelves (P8); screws better for long shelf (P7)
→ Recommended: Finish nails (fast, minimal holes for paint)
⚙️ INSTALLATION - Vertical Down Through Shelf:
  1. PRE-DRILL: Use 1/8" countersink bit through TOP surface of shelf (3 holes per cleat: left, center, right)
  2. COUNTERSINK: Creates small recess for nail/screw head to sit flush (will be painted)
  3. DRIVE: 1.25"-1.5" fasteners vertically DOWN through shelf into cleat below
  4. POSITIONING:
    • Left fastener: ~2" from left edge of shelf
    • Center fastener: At middle of cleat (10" for standard cleat)
    • Right fastener: ~2" from right edge of shelf
🟠 FASTENERS - Shelves to Vertical Panels (Horizontal Fastening):
  • Use: 1.5"-2" finish nails OR 1.625" trim screws × 2-3 per shelf side
  • These are supplementary to vertical fasteners
  • Nails work well for these secondary connections
⚙️ INSTALLATION - Horizontal Through Side Panels:
  1. PRE-DRILL: Use 1/8" pilot bit through side panel into shelf edge (2-3 holes per side)
  2. DRIVE: 1.5"-2" fasteners HORIZONTALLY through the side panels (P4, P5) or divider (P6) into shelf edge
  3. PLACEMENT:
    • One fastener near left edge of shelf
    • One fastener at center of shelf
    • One fastener near right edge of shelf (optional)
  4. ANGLE: Aim fasteners into center of plywood edge to avoid splitting
⏱️ CLAMPING & DRYING: Consider using light clamps (spring clamps or quick-grip) to hold shelves level while glue cures. Wait 30 minutes before loading shelves with items.
Figure D: All shelves (P7 & P8) positioned and secured on mounting cleats (P10)
5
Attach Top & Bottom Panels [P1 & P2]
Parts Used:
[P1] Top Panel (92" × 23.25")
[P2] Bottom Panel with Skids (92" × 23.25")
[P4] [P5] [P6] Vertical panels

Instructions - Attach Top Panel [P1]:

  1. Apply glue: Use Titebond II continuous 1/4" bead to the TOP EDGES of all three vertical panels (P4, P5, P6). This is critical for structural integrity.
  2. Carefully place P1 (Top Panel) squarely on top of the frame, ensuring:
    • Front edges are aligned and flush
    • Panel is level (use a level tool)
    • Overhang is equal on all sides (approximately equal on left, right, front, back)
  3. Clamp: Use bar clamps or C-clamps to hold P1 firmly against the frame tops. See detailed clamping diagram below.
  4. Let glue tack 10 minutes before driving fasteners.
  5. Pre-drill pilot holes with 1/8" countersink bit through P1 into each vertical panel (3 holes per panel).
  6. Drive 2" #8 wood screws vertically down through P1 into the top edge of each panel.

Final Assembly Notes & Safety

Weight Warning: When fully assembled with all plywood and hardware, this cabinet weighs approximately 160 lbs. Always use 3-4 people to lift, move, or flip the cabinet frame. Never attempt alone.

Installation Location

  • Assemble this shelf unit inside or immediately adjacent to the chemistry lab room where it will permanently stay.
  • Place on a level, flat floor.
  • DO NOT WALL MOUNT - This shelf is engineered to be completely free-standing.
  • The internal cleats, back panel, and base skids provide all structural rigidity needed.
  • Ensure floor is clean and level before final placement.

Before Loading Supplies

  • Allow all finishes to fully cure (24-48 hours) before placing any items on shelves.
  • Verify all fasteners are tight and secure.
  • Distribute load evenly across shelves - do not overload single areas.
  • Keep heavier items on lower shelves.

Maintenance

  • Clean shelves regularly with damp cloth and mild soap (suitable for lab environments).
  • Dry immediately to prevent water damage.
  • Check fasteners periodically and tighten if needed.
  • Do not drag or slide on floor - always lift when moving.
