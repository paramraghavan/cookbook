# Fairlife Vanilla Ice Cream

## For a 24 oz Ninja CREAMi Deluxe Container

### Ingredients

- 2 cups plain Fairlife 2% milk
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract  
  **or** 1 teaspoon vanilla bean paste
- 2 tablespoons cooked white rice
- Small pinch of salt

### Why Add Cooked Rice?

The cooked rice helps make the ice cream:

- Creamier
- Less icy
- Smoother without needing heavy cream or pudding mix

Start with **2 tablespoons of cooked rice**. You can use up to **3 tablespoons** for extra creaminess, but too much may make the ice cream thick or starchy.

### Instructions

1. Add the Fairlife milk, sugar, vanilla, cooked rice, and salt to a blender.
2. Blend until completely smooth, with no visible rice pieces.
3. Pour the mixture into the Ninja CREAMi Deluxe container.
4. Do not go above the **MAX FILL** line.
5. Freeze the container flat for about **24 hours**.
6. Process using the **Lite Ice Cream** setting.
7. If the ice cream looks powdery or crumbly, add **1–2 tablespoons of Fairlife milk**.
8. Use **Re-Spin** until smooth and creamy.

## Tip

For the best texture, make sure the cooked rice is very soft and blended thoroughly into the milk before freezing.
