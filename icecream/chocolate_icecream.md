# Fairlife Chocolate Ice Cream

## For 24 oz Ninja CREAMi Deluxe XL Container

### Ingredients

- 2 1/4 cups Fairlife chocolate milk
- 2-3 tablespoons very soft cooked white rice
- 1 1/2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1-2 tablespoons sugar or maple syrup, optional
- Small pinch of salt

### Instructions

1. Add the Fairlife chocolate milk, cooked rice, cocoa powder, vanilla, salt, and optional sweetener to a blender.
2. Blend very well until completely smooth and no rice grains remain.
3. Pour into the 24 oz Ninja CREAMi Deluxe container. Do not go above the MAX FILL line.
4. Freeze flat for at least 24 hours.
5. Process using **Lite Ice Cream**.
6. If it looks dry or crumbly, add 1-2 tablespoons Fairlife chocolate milk.
7. Run **Re-Spin**.
8. Re-Spin once more if you want an even smoother texture.

### For Extra Creaminess

You can add **2 tablespoons heavy cream** before freezing.

### Tip

Use about **3 tablespoons cooked rice** for this size. Adding much more may make the ice cream taste starchy or become
slightly gummy. It adds starch, which helps the Fairlife chocolate milk freeze into something creamier and less icy. It
also gives the mixture a little more body, similar to what pudding mix or a commercial stabilizer would do.
