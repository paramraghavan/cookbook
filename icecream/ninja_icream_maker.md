# Simple Ice Cream Recipes using Ninja CREAMi Deluxe XL

> For the **24 oz Ninja CREAMi Deluxe XL container**, optimized for **Kirkland ultra-filtered milk** or **full-fat
regular milk (whole milk)**.

## Quick Rules

- **Ice cream / scoopable:** Fill only up to the **SCOOPABLE MAX FILL** line.
- **Drinkable recipes:** Freeze only up to **DRINKABLE FREEZE FILL**; after freezing, add the specified liquid up to the
  **POUR-IN** line.
- Freeze the container **flat and level for 24 hours**.
- You **do not have to fill to MAX FILL**. A smaller batch is fine.
- If ice cream is dry or powdery after processing, add **1–2 tbsp milk** and **RE-SPIN**.
- Never exceed the appropriate fill line.

> **Easy rule:** For ice cream, use the **SCOOPABLE MAX FILL** line and ignore the drinkable lines.

---

## Milk Options: Ultra-Filtered vs. Full-Fat Whole Milk

- **Kirkland Ultra-Filtered Milk:** Naturally higher in protein and lower in lactose, giving a very smooth texture. Use
  the **LITE ICE CREAM** setting.
- **Full-Fat Regular Milk (Whole Milk):** Offers a traditional high-fat, rich dairy flavor. If using whole milk, you can
  use either the **ICE CREAM** or **LITE ICE CREAM** setting depending on how rich you want the final texture.

## Special Techniques for Coffee & Chocolate Chip

### ☕ Elevating Coffee Ice Cream

Cold dairy dulls bitter and rich notes. To get a true coffeehouse flavor:

1. Dissolve your **instant espresso or coffee** in **1 tablespoon of warm water** *before* whisking it into the cold
   milk and sugar.
2. *Pro-Tip:* Add a tiny pinch of salt; it acts as a flavor amplifier that makes the coffee taste deeper and richer.

### 🍪 Crafting Chocolate Chip (The Mix-In Method)

Do not blend the chocolate chips into the base before freezing, or they will pulverize into dust and ruin your blade.

1. Freeze your base (Vanilla or Chocolate) normally for 24 hours.
2. Process on **FULL → ICE CREAM** or **LITE ICE CREAM**.
3. Use a spoon to create a 1-inch wide hole down to the bottom of the pint (the "well" method).
4. Drop in **⅓ cup of mini chocolate chips**.
5. Re-insert the pint into the outer bowl and select **MIX-IN**.

## Fruit Notes & Easy Fruit Formula

> **Pineapple:** Pineapple ice cream tasted bitter in our test due to active enzymes reacting with dairy, so pineapple
> is **not recommended**.

> **Frozen Berries:** Blend frozen fruit, milk, and sugar until completely smooth **before freezing**. Do not freeze
> loose chunks of fruit by themselves.

### Easy Fruit Formula (Smaller Batch)

**1 cup fruit + 1 cup Milk (Ultra-Filtered or Whole Milk) + about 2 tbsp sugar + pinch of salt**

1. Blend the **fruit + milk + sugar + salt** until completely smooth.
2. Pour into the CREAMi container (it's fine if it's below the max line).
3. Freeze flat for **24 hours**.
4. Process using **FULL → ICE CREAM** or **LITE ICE CREAM**.
5. If powdery, add **1–2 tbsp milk → RE-SPIN**.

---

## Basic Method — Same for Every Ice Cream

1. **Whisk or blend** all ingredients until fully smooth and dissolved.
2. Pour into the container, staying at or below **SCOOPABLE MAX FILL**.
3. Put on the storage lid securely.
4. Freeze **flat and level for 24 hours**.
5. Remove lid and place the container in the outer bowl.
6. Secure outer bowl lid with the blade, lock into the machine, and select **FULL → ICE CREAM** (for whole milk bases)
   or **LITE ICE CREAM** (for ultra-filtered milk bases).
7. If the texture is crumbly or powdery, add **1–2 tbsp milk** and run a **RE-SPIN**.
8. Serve immediately.

## Pro-Tips — Make It Extra Creamy

* **The Pudding Trick (Before Freezing):** Whisk **1 tbsp instant pudding mix** directly into your liquid base **before
  freezing** (Vanilla for vanilla/fruit/coffee; Chocolate for chocolate). This acts as a powerful thickener and
  stabilizer as it freezes.
* **The Rice Thickener Trick (Before Freezing):** Have leftover cooked rice? Blend **1 cup cooked rice** with 1.5–2 cups
  of milk and sugar until completely smooth **before freezing** to use natural starches as a stabilizer.
* **The Cream Cheese Secret (Before Freezing):** Blend **1 tbsp softened cream cheese** into your liquid base before
  freezing. It adds the milkfat structure needed to prevent icy crystals entirely (especially great if using lower-fat
  milks, though optional with whole milk).
