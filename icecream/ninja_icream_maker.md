# Simple Ice Cream Recipes using Ninja CREAMi Deluxe XL

For the **24 oz Ninja CREAMi Deluxe XL container**, especially with **Kirkland ultra-filtered milk**.

## Quick Rules

- **Ice cream / scoopable:** Fill only up to the **SCOOPABLE MAX FILL** line.
- **Drinkable recipes:** Freeze only up to **DRINKABLE FREEZE FILL**; after freezing, add the specified liquid up to the
  **POUR-IN** line.
- Freeze the container **flat and level for 24 hours**.
- You **do not have to fill to MAX FILL**. A smaller batch is fine.
- If ice cream is dry or powdery after processing, add **1–2 tbsp milk** and **RE-SPIN**.
- Never exceed the appropriate fill line.

> **Easy rule:** For ice cream, use the **SCOOPABLE MAX FILL** line and ignore the drinkable lines.

## Simple Recipes

| Flavor               | Kirkland Ultra-Filtered Milk | Add-ins                                              |
|----------------------|-----------------------------:|------------------------------------------------------|
| 🍦 **Vanilla**       |                      2½ cups | 3 tbsp sugar + 1 tsp vanilla                         |
| 🍫 **Chocolate**     |                      2½ cups | 3 tbsp sugar + 2 tbsp cocoa + ½–1 tsp vanilla        |
| 🥭 **Mango**         |                       2 cups | ½ cup mango pulp + 1–2 tbsp sugar                    |
| 🍓 **Strawberry**    |                       2 cups | ½ cup mashed strawberries + 2–3 tbsp sugar           |
| ☕ **Coffee**         |                      2½ cups | 3 tbsp sugar + 1½ tsp instant coffee + ½ tsp vanilla |
| 🍌 **Banana**        |                       2 cups | 1 ripe banana + 1–2 tbsp sugar                       |
| 🫐 **Blueberry**     |                        1 cup | 1 cup frozen blueberries + 2 tbsp sugar              |
| 🍑 **Peach**         |                        1 cup | 1 cup frozen peaches + 2 tbsp sugar                  |
| 🍒 **Cherry**        |                        1 cup | 1 cup pitted cherries + 2 tbsp sugar                 |
| 🫐🍓 **Mixed Berry** |                        1 cup | 1 cup frozen mixed berries + 2 tbsp sugar            |

### Fruit Notes

> **Pineapple:** Pineapple ice cream tasted bitter in our test, so pineapple is **not recommended** in this recipe list.

> **Frozen berries and other frozen fruit:** You **do not need to crush them first** if you are using a blender. Put the
> frozen fruit, milk, and sugar in the blender and blend until smooth **before freezing the CREAMi container**. If you are
> not using a blender, thaw the fruit slightly and mash/crush it well first.

### Easy Fruit Formula

For a simple smaller batch:

**1 cup fruit + 1 cup Kirkland ultra-filtered milk + about 2 tbsp sugar**

Yes — this works well with **blueberries, peaches, strawberries, mango, cherries, or mixed berries**.

1. Blend the **fruit + milk + sugar** until smooth.
2. Pour into the CREAMi container. It is perfectly fine if it is **below SCOOPABLE MAX FILL**.
3. Freeze flat for **24 hours**.
4. Process using **FULL → LITE ICE CREAM**.
5. If powdery, add **1–2 tbsp milk → RE-SPIN**.

> **Frozen fruit:** Blend it with the milk before freezing the CREAMi base. Do not freeze loose pieces of fruit by
> themselves as the ice-cream base.

### Adjusting Sweetness

Taste the mixture **before freezing**. Frozen desserts taste less sweet when frozen, so the liquid base should taste
slightly sweeter than you want the finished ice cream.

- Very sweet fruit: **1 tbsp sugar may be enough**
- Average fruit: **2 tbsp sugar**
- Tart berries: **2–3 tbsp sugar**

## Basic Method — Same for Every Ice Cream

1. Mix or blend ingredients until smooth.
2. Pour into the container, staying at or below **SCOOPABLE MAX FILL**.
3. Put on the storage lid.
4. Freeze **flat for 24 hours**.
5. Place the container in the outer bowl.
6. Select **FULL → LITE ICE CREAM**.
7. If powdery, add **1–2 tbsp milk → RE-SPIN**.
8. Serve immediately.

---

## Optional — Make It Creamier

For a creamier texture, add **1 tbsp instant pudding mix** before freezing.

- Vanilla pudding → vanilla, fruit, banana or coffee
- Chocolate pudding → chocolate

This is optional.

## Fill-Line Cheat Sheet

| What You're Making             | Before Freezing                                | After Freezing                              |
|--------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Ice Cream / Lite Ice Cream** | Anywhere below or up to **SCOOPABLE MAX FILL** | Nothing required                            |
| **Slushi / Frozen Drink**      | Up to **DRINKABLE FREEZE FILL**                | Add specified liquid up to **POUR-IN** line |

## Remember

**Ice cream = SCOOPABLE MAX FILL**

**Drinkable = FREEZE FILL → freeze → POUR-IN**

**Freeze flat = 24 hours**
