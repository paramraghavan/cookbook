This podcast episode from *Body to Beiing* featuring Udit Chawla (co-founder of WellWith) offers a refreshing,
scientifically grounded perspective on ancient Ayurvedic botany. Rather than treating Ayurveda as mystical folklore, the
discussion bridges traditional concepts—like *Rasayanas* (rejuvenators) and *Ama* (toxic bio-accumulation)—with modern
extraction techniques and clinical markers (such as beta-cell regeneration, cortisol suppression, and anthocyanin
density).

The standout insight of the video is the emphasis on **botanical bio-availability**—such as differentiating between Sea
Buckthorn berry oil versus seed oil, or whole turmeric oil versus isolated curcumin powder. For health seekers in the
US, these herbs provide targeted support against Western lifestyle stressors: chronic stress, metabolic dysfunction,
internal heat/inflammation, and gut dysbiosis.

---

## Complete Herb List: Benefits, Usage, US Availability & Best Supplement Brands

### 1. Sea Buckthorn (*Hippophae rhamnoides* / Modern Sanjeevani)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** Highly rich in Omega-3, 6, 9, and the rare Omega-7. Pacifies *Vata* and *Pitta* while
  nourishing *Mamsa* (muscle) and *Rasa* (plasma) tissue.
* **How to Use:**
* **Berry Oil:** Rich in Omega-7; ideal for restoring mucous membranes (gut lining, dry eye, vaginal dryness), skin
  hydration, and natural UV protection.
* **Seed Oil:** Rich in Omega-3 & 6; best for cardiovascular health, arterial elasticity, and balancing lipid levels.


* **US Market Application:** Excellent for dry eye syndrome, acid reflux/leaky gut, and anti-aging skin health.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **SeabuckWonders – Sea Buckthorn Omega-7 Complete** (A blended softgel containing
  both berry and seed oils).

---

### 2. Ashwagandha (*Withania somnifera*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** Potent *Balya* (strength-promoter) and adaptogen. Calms hyperactive *Vata* in the nervous
  system.
* **How to Use:** Take in the evening or before bed with warm water or warm plant milk to reduce serum cortisol,
  decrease stress-induced visceral (belly) fat, and promote deep non-REM sleep.
* **US Market Application:** Perfect for high-cortisol, high-stress professional lifestyles, anxiety, and insomnia.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Youtheory Ashwagandha** or **Organic India Ashwagandha** (KSM-66 full-spectrum
  root extract).

---

### 3. Brahmi (*Bacopa monnieri*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** Premier *Medhya Rasayana* (brain tonic). Balances *Sadhaka Pitta* (emotional brain) and *Prana
  Vata* (mental flow).
* **How to Use:** Take daily in the morning or early afternoon with a meal (preferably containing healthy fats for
  absorption). Enhances synaptic communication, working memory, and mental stamina while calming cognitive fatigue.
* **US Market Application:** Ideal for nootropic bio-hacking, ADHD-like brain fog, and long-term neuroprotection.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Himalaya Organic Bacopa / Brahmi** or **Organic India Brahmi-Gotu Kola**.

---

### 4. Punarnava (*Boerhavia diffusa*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** Translated as *"that which renews the body."* Powerful *Mootrala* (diuretic) and kidney
  rejuvenate herb that balances *Kapha* and *Pitta*.
* **How to Use:** Take twice daily with warm water. Flushes systemic fluid accumulation, clears lymphatic stagnation,
  aids kidney filtration, and reduces puffiness under the eyes.
* **US Market Application:** Excellent natural remedy for fluid retention, mild hypertension, urinary tract support, and
  kidney detox.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Banyan Botanicals Punarnava** or **Planet Ayurveda Punarnava Mandur**.

---

### 5. Jamun Seed Extract (*Syzygium cumini*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** *Astringent (Kashaya)* and cooling. Directly targets *Meda Dhatu* (fat tissue) and *Mutra* (
  urinary channel).
* **How to Use:** Take 15–30 minutes before carbohydrate-heavy meals. Active compounds like jamboline inhibit
  alpha-amylase enzymes, preventing sudden post-meal blood sugar spikes and encouraging pancreatic beta-cell recovery.
* **US Market Application:** Crucial for metabolic syndrome, pre-diabetes, and Type-2 diabetes management.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Planetary Herbals Jamun Seed Extract** or **Organic India Blood Sugar Care** (
  featuring Jamun and Karela).

---

### 6. Kokum (*Garcinia indica*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** Deeply cooling (*Sheeta*) and digestive (*Deepana*). Instantly quenches excess *Pitta* (heat)
  in the stomach.
* **How to Use:** Traditionally consumed as a infusion or rinds, but in extract form, it suppresses hydroxycitric acid (
  HCA)-driven lipid synthesis, alleviates severe acidity, and curbs hyper-reactive sugar cravings.
* **US Market Application:** Natural relief for chronic heartburn, acid reflux, summer heat exhaustion, and weight
  management.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Planet Ayurveda Garcinia/Kokum** or **Pure Indian Foods Organic Kokum Extract
  Capsules**.

---

### 7. Ashok Chaal / Ashoka Bark (*Saraca asoca*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** Meaning *"remover of sorrow."* The gold-standard uterine tonic in Ayurveda, reducing excess
  *Pitta* and blood heat.
* **How to Use:** Take during the luteal phase or throughout the cycle to tone uterine musculature, ease dysmenorrhea (
  cramping), and control abnormally heavy menstrual flow (menorrhagia).
* **US Market Application:** High utility for women managing PCOS, PCOD, endometriosis symptoms, or irregular cycles.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Vadik Herbs Wildcrafted Ashoka Bark Capsules** or **Garry N Sun Ashoka Capsules
  **.

---

### 8. Shatavari (*Asparagus racemosus*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** The primary female adaptogen (*"she who possesses a hundred husbands"*). Deeply tonifies
  *Shukra Dhatu* (reproductive tissue).
* **How to Use:** Take morning and evening with warm milk, water, or coconut oil to support endocrine function, boost
  libido, and soothe hormonal mood swings.
* **US Market Application:** Endocrine balance for PMS, postpartum recovery, perimenopause, and menopausal hot flashes.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Organic India Shatavari** or **Banyan Botanicals Shatavari**.

---

### 9. Black Rice Extract (*Oryza sativa* / Forbidden Rice)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** Rich in anthocyanins (dense purple/black pigments) that clear deep *Ama* (oxidative debris) at
  a cellular level.
* **How to Use:** Taken with meals as a concentrated extract. Supplies a far higher antioxidant payload than
  blueberries, fighting free radicals, supporting arterial walls, and improving gut lining cell turnover.
* **US Market Application:** Premium systemic anti-aging, vascular support, and gut mucosal healing.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Vital Nutrients Anthocyanin / Black Rice Extract** or **Piping Rock Black Rice
  Extract**.

---

### 10. Full-Spectrum Turmeric & Turmeric Oil (*Curcuma longa*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** *Krimighna* (antimicrobial) and *Vishaghna* (antitoxin). Balances all three Doshas.
* **How to Use:** Standard curcumin powder lacks high absorption without lipids. Combining curcumin with **turmerones
  ** (turmeric essential oil) drastically improves bio-availability to clear joint inflammation and repair intestinal
  permeability.
* **US Market Application:** Systemic anti-inflammatory, joint pain relief, and autoimmune/gut support.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Terry Naturally CuraMed** (Utilizes BCM-95, combining curcumin with natural
  turmeric essential oils/turmerones for high absorption).

---

### 11. Giloy / Guduchi (*Tinospora cordifolia*)

* **Ayurvedic Profile:** Known as *Amrita* (Heavenly Nectar). A rare *Tridoshic* immunomodulator.
* **How to Use:** Take during seasonal changes or immune challenges to purify the blood (*Rakta Shodhana*), support
  liver detoxification, and clear lingering low-grade inflammation/fevers.
* **US Market Application:** Daily immune system defense, allergy management, and liver detox.
* **Best US Pill/Supplement Version:** **Organic India Guduchi / Giloy** or **Banyan Botanicals Guduchi**.
