# Ajwain–Jeera–Saunf Digestive Spice Powder

## Ingredients

Use this ratio:

- **Ajwain (Carom Seeds):** 4 parts
- **Jeera (Cumin Seeds):** 2 parts
- **Saunf (Fennel Seeds):** 1 part
- **Elaichi (Cardamom):** ½ part

### Example Batch

- 4 tbsp Ajwain
- 2 tbsp Jeera
- 1 tbsp Saunf
- ½ tbsp Cardamom

## Preparation

1. **Dry roast** the ajwain, jeera, and saunf together in a pan until warm and aromatic.
2. Remove from the heat.
3. Add the **cardamom**.
4. Grind everything together into a **fine powder**.
5. Store in an airtight container.

## How to Take

- Take **1 teaspoon** of the powder with **warm water** at night before sleeping.

## Claimed Benefit

The recipe is traditionally suggested for relieving **general body pain and discomfort**.

> **Note:** This is a traditional/home remedy. Evidence that this spice mixture treats body pain is limited, so it shouldn't replace medical evaluation or treatment for persistent or significant pain.
