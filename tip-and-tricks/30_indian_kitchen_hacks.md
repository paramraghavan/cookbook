# 30 Indian kitchen hacks

1. **Prevent Milk Boiling Over:** Lay a wooden spoon flat across the top of the pot. When the milk foams up and touches the wood, the bubbles burst, buying you time to lower the heat.
2. **Prevent Milk from Sticking/Burning:** Rinse the empty pan with cold water right before pouring in the milk without drying it.
3. **Keep Fresh Herbs Fresh for Weeks:** Do not wash cilantro, mint, or parsley before storing. Place them in a container lined with paper towels on the top and bottom to absorb excess moisture.
4. **Extend the Shelf Life of Green Chilies:** Remove the little green stem from every chili before storing them in a container lined with paper towels.
5. **Keep Green Chutney Bright Green:** Add a squeeze of lemon juice or a tablespoon of plain yogurt while blending to preserve its vibrant color for 5–7 days.
6. **Eliminate Slimy Okra (Bhindi):** Add something sour (lemon/lime juice, vinegar, or *amchur* dry mango powder) within 5 to 10 minutes of cooking the okra in the pan.
7. **Absorb Fridge Odors:** Place an open dish filled with 1 cup of baking soda inside the fridge, and replace it every 2 to 3 months.
8. **Keep Bugs Out of Flour & Grains:** Place 1 or 2 dried bay leaves into jars of flour, rice, or lentils to repel pantry pests.
9. **Store Bananas Separately:** Keep bananas away from other fruits so their natural ethylene gas doesn't spoil surrounding produce prematurely (or pair a banana with hard avocados/pears to ripen them overnight).
10. **Prevent Yogurt Curry (Kadhi) from Splitting:** Stir continuously until the first boil, let it simmer on low heat, and add salt only at the very end.
11. **Quick-Soak Forgot-to-Soak Beans:** Soak chickpeas or beans in warm water for a couple of hours and add a tiny pinch of baking soda to the cooking water to soften them quickly.
12. **Stop Pressure Cooker Spitting:** Never fill the cooker over half full, let the food boil without the lid first, and add a few drops of oil to keep foam down.
13. **Make Two Roti at Once:** Roll two small balls of dough into rounds, brush oil and dry flour between them, layer them together, roll, and cook both at once before peeling them apart.
14. **Softer Roti Dough:** Knead the dough using milk instead of water to keep the rotis soft longer in lunchboxes.
15. **Easiest Garlic Peeling:** Microwave whole garlic cloves for 20 seconds, or soak them in warm water for 5 to 7 minutes to make the skins slide off effortlessly.
16. **Control Garlic Flavor:** Slice garlic for mild flavor, chop finely for moderate flavor, or crush/paste it for the strongest garlic taste.
17. **Brown Onions Faster:** Add a tiny pinch of baking soda to onions in the pan to turn them soft and golden brown much faster.
18. **Non-Greasy Puris:** Knead a stiffer dough and rest the rolled puris in the refrigerator for 15 minutes before deep frying.
19. **Restaurant-Style Flavor in Paneer:** Rub crushed *kasuri methi* (dried fenugreek leaves) between your palms over the curry right at the end of cooking.
20. **Cook Kheer Faster:** Start cooking the rice in water mixed with a little milk until half-done, then pour in the full-cream milk to finish.
21. **Reuse Frying Oil Safely:** Do not deep fry with used oil a second time; strain it cold through a fine cloth and reserve it for curries, dals, or parathas.
22. **Better Homemade Pizza Crust:** Combine equal parts whole wheat flour, semolina (*sooji*), and plain flour seasoned with Italian herbs, salt, and olive oil.
23. **Sneak Vegetables into Parathas:** Knead boiled & blended beetroot (pink) or blanched & blended spinach (green) into dough to make colorful parathas.
24. **Store Vegetables in Mesh/Cotton Bags:** Use breathable fabric or mesh bags rather than plastic to let air circulate and keep produce fresh longer.
25. **Soft Butter in a Minute:** Invert a warm, clean glass or mug (heated via microwave or hot water) over hard butter on a plate to soften it quickly.
26. **Remove Sticky Jar Labels Easily:** Blow a warm hair dryer over the sticker label for 20 to 30 seconds to melt the glue before peeling.
27. **Wash Berries & Fruit Thoroughly:** Soak fruits/berries in water mixed with a few drops of lemon juice for a few minutes before rinsing to lift dirt and germs.
28. **Repurpose Squeezed Lemons:** Rub leftover lemon peels around the kitchen sink for freshness, dip in salt to scrub wooden cutting boards, or clean copper pots.
29. **Morning Cumin Water Routine:** Start your day with a warm glass of water boiled with cumin seeds (*jeera*) for a gentle morning digestif.
30. **Clean the Blackest, Greasiest Pans:** Submerge the burnt pan inside a large pot of boiling water mixed with 1 cup of baking soda, turn off heat, and leave it overnight before scrubbing clean with steel wool the next morning.
