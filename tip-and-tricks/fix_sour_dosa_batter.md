1. Take 3 tbsp cooked rice.
2. Add 2 tbsp water and mash or blend into a smooth paste.
3. Add the paste to 2 cups dosa batter.
4. Mix well.
5. If the batter is still sour, add 1 tbsp rice flour.
6. Mix again; add a little water only if the batter becomes too thick.
> cooked rice make dosa soft as well
