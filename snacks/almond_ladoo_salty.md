# Soft, Buttery Salted Almond–Cashew Ladoo
> I have never had salted ladoo

A savory, melt-in-the-mouth ladoo made with almond powder, cashew powder, ghee, and traditional Indian spices. No sugar, jaggery, or milk required.

**Preparation time:** 5 minutes  
**Cooking time:** 10 minutes  
**Yield:** 10–12 small ladoos  
**Diet:** Vegetarian, egg-free, gluten-free (if using gluten-free hing)

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fine almond powder | 75 g | Prefer blanched almond flour |
| Raw cashews | 25 g | Grind into fine powder |
| Ghee | 25–35 g | Start with 25 g |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | Adjust to taste |
| Roasted cumin powder | ½ tsp | Jeera |
| Black pepper powder | ¼ tsp | Adjust to taste |
| Hing (asafoetida) | A pinch | Optional |
| Dried curry leaves | ½ tsp | Finely crushed, optional |
| Smooth unsweetened cashew butter | 5–10 g | Optional, for stronger binding |

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Cashew Powder

1. Use raw, unsalted cashews.
2. Pulse briefly in a mixer until finely powdered.
3. Avoid continuous grinding, which can turn the cashews into butter.
4. For stronger binding, optionally replace 5–10 g of the cashew powder with an equal weight of smooth cashew butter.

### 2. Roast the Almond and Cashew Powders

1. Heat a heavy-bottomed pan over very low heat.
2. Add almond powder and cashew powder.
3. Dry roast for 2–3 minutes, stirring continuously.
4. Stop when the mixture becomes fragrant. Do not brown.

### 3. Add Ghee

1. Add 25 g ghee.
2. Mix continuously for 1–2 minutes on low heat.
3. Add optional cashew butter, if using.
4. Stir until evenly combined.
5. Turn off the heat.

### 4. Add Seasonings

1. Add salt, roasted cumin powder, black pepper, and hing.
2. Add finely crushed dried curry leaves if desired.
3. Mix thoroughly while the mixture is warm.

### 5. Shape the Ladoos

1. Allow the mixture to cool until comfortably warm.
2. Take a small portion and squeeze firmly in your palm.
3. If it holds together, roll gently into a ball.
4. If crumbly, add warm ghee ½ teaspoon at a time.
5. Shape into 10–12 small ladoos.

### 6. Allow to Set

1. Arrange ladoos on a plate.
2. Let them rest for 20–30 minutes.
3. Serve at room temperature.

## Tips for Soft, Melt-in-the-Mouth Ladoos

- **Fine almond flour:** Use superfine blanched almond flour for a smooth texture.
- **Cashew powder:** Adds richness and creaminess, although it is not a strong binder by itself.
- **Cashew butter:** Helps bind more effectively than dry cashew powder.
- **Ghee:** Add gradually to avoid greasy ladoos.
- **Low-temperature roasting:** Prevents the nuts from becoming bitter or excessively toasted.
- **Warm shaping:** Shape while warm because ghee becomes firmer as it cools.
- **Small ladoos:** Smaller portions hold together more easily.

## Almond-to-Cashew Ratios

| Almond | Cashew | Expected Texture |
|---|---|---|
| 90 g | 10 g | Strong almond flavor, more crumbly |
| **75 g** | **25 g** | Creamy, balanced flavor |
| 50 g | 50 g | Richer cashew flavor, potentially more cohesive |

**Recommended:** 75:25 almond-to-cashew ratio.

## Flavor Variations

### Jeera Pepper Ladoo
Use ½ tsp roasted cumin powder and ¼ tsp black pepper for a mild, savory flavor.

### Sesame Almond–Cashew Ladoo
Add 1 tsp finely ground roasted sesame seeds for additional nuttiness.

### South Indian Kara Ladoo
Add ⅛ tsp red chili powder, a pinch of hing, and ½ tsp crushed dried curry leaves.

## Storage

- Store in an airtight container.
- Refrigerate for up to 1–2 weeks.
- Allow refrigerated ladoos to come to room temperature before serving for a softer texture.
- Avoid adding milk or water if longer storage is desired.

## Recommended Final Formula

For the best balance of softness, flavor, and binding:

- 75 g almond powder
- 20 g cashew powder
- 5 g smooth cashew butter
- 25–30 g ghee, adjusted as needed
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- Pinch of hing

**Result:** A rich, buttery, mildly spiced almond–cashew ladoo with a soft texture and improved binding without sugar.
