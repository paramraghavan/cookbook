# Indian Sweets – Sugar & Jaggery Syrup Guide

> **Best rule:** Use the **string/water test as the main guide** and the thermometer as confirmation.
> Exact temperature can vary slightly with thermometer accuracy, altitude, pan, and jaggery type.

---

## Quick Reference

| Sweet                      | Syrup Stage           |              Approx. Temp | Easy Test          | Ready When                          |
|----------------------------|-----------------------|--------------------------:|--------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Boondi Ladoo**           | 1-string              | **230–235°F / 110–113°C** | Finger string test | One thin string forms               |
| **Coconut Burfi**          | 1-string to soft-ball | **235–240°F / 113–116°C** | Cold-water test    | Soft ball forms and flattens easily |
| **Kaju Katli**             | 1-string              | **230–235°F / 110–113°C** | Finger string test | One thin string forms               |
| **Peanut Ladoo – Jaggery** | Soft-ball             | **235–240°F / 113–116°C** | Cold-water test    | Soft, pliable ball forms            |

# How to Test the Syrup

## 1-String Test

Best for:

* **Boondi ladoo**
* **Kaju katli**
* Many traditional Indian sweets

### How

1. Dip a clean spoon into the syrup.
2. Put **one small drop** on the spoon.
3. Wait about **5–10 seconds** so it is warm, not boiling hot.
4. Lightly wet your thumb and index finger.
5. Touch a tiny amount of the cooled syrup between them.
6. Press the fingers together once.
7. Slowly pull them apart.

### Ready

A **thin single string** forms between your fingers before breaking.

### Not Ready

If the syrup is only sticky and no string forms:

* Cook another **30–60 seconds**.
* Test again.

> **Safety:** Never touch syrup directly from the boiling pan. Hot sugar syrup can cause serious burns.

---

## Soft-Ball Test

Best for:

* **Coconut burfi**
* **Peanut/jaggery ladoo**

### How

1. Keep a small bowl of **cold water** beside the stove.
2. Drop about **¼ teaspoon syrup** into the water.
3. Wait a few seconds.
4. Gather the syrup gently with your fingers.

### Ready

The syrup should form a **soft ball** that:

* Holds together
* Feels soft and pliable
* Flattens easily when pressed

### Not Ready

If it:

* **Dissolves completely:** cook longer.
* **Cannot be gathered:** syrup is still too thin.
* **Forms a firm or hard ball:** syrup has cooked too far.

# Sweet-Specific Tips

## Boondi Ladoo

Target: **1-string syrup**

The syrup should be:

* Slightly thick
* Sticky
* Able to form one thin string
* Still fluid enough to coat the boondi

### Important

Do **not** cook the syrup until soft-ball stage.
Overcooked syrup can make the ladoos:

* Hard
* Dry
* Difficult to shape

After mixing boondi with syrup, let it sit briefly until it is **warm enough to handle**, then shape the ladoos.

---

## Coconut Burfi

Target: **1-string to soft-ball**

After adding coconut, watch the mixture rather than relying only on temperature.

It is usually ready when:

* Mixture thickens
* It begins leaving the sides of the pan
* It gathers together
* It can still be spread into a tray

Do not cook until completely dry or the burfi may become hard.

---

## Kaju Katli

Target: **1-string syrup**

After adding the cashew powder, cook until the mixture:

* Thickens
* Leaves the sides of the pan
* Forms a soft mass
* Is no longer very sticky

### Quick dough test

Take a tiny piece and let it cool for a few seconds.

Try rolling it between your fingers.

**Ready:** it forms a smooth, soft ball without sticking badly.

---

## Peanut Ladoo with Jaggery

Target: **soft-ball stage**

Use the **cold-water test** because jaggery color and consistency can vary.

The jaggery syrup should:

* Form a soft ball in cold water
* Hold together when pressed
* Remain pliable

Do not wait until it becomes hard or brittle.

# Troubleshooting

| Problem                       | Likely Cause                             | What to Do                                          |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Ladoo will not hold together  | Syrup undercooked                        | Cook a little longer and test again                 |
| Ladoo becomes hard            | Syrup overcooked                         | Add **1–2 tbsp hot water**, warm gently and re-test |
| Syrup sticky but no string    | Slightly undercooked                     | Cook **30–60 seconds** more                         |
| Cold-water syrup dissolves    | Too thin                                 | Continue cooking                                    |
| Soft-ball becomes firm/hard   | Overcooked                               | Add a little hot water and gently reheat            |
| Sugar crystallizes            | Sugar on pan sides or too much agitation | Add a little water and dissolve gently              |
| Jaggery turns very thick/hard | Overcooked                               | Add a little hot water and warm gently              |

# Thermometer Check

Before making sweets:

1. Bring plain water to a rolling boil.
2. Thermometer should read about **212°F / 100°C at sea level**.
3. Note any difference.
4. Keep the thermometer tip **inside the syrup but away from the bottom and sides of the pan**.

### Example

If boiling water reads **210°F instead of 212°F**, your thermometer reads about **2°F low**.

Use the syrup tests as the final confirmation rather than chasing an exact number.

# Useful Candy Stages

| Stage                 |       Approx. Temperature | Simple Meaning                    |
|-----------------------|--------------------------:|-----------------------------------|
| **1-String / Thread** | **230–235°F / 110–113°C** | Thin string forms between fingers |
| **Soft Ball**         | **235–240°F / 113–116°C** | Soft ball forms in cold water     |
| **Firm Ball**         | **245–250°F / 118–121°C** | Firm but still bendable ball      |
| **Hard Ball**         | **250–265°F / 121–129°C** | Hard, compact ball                |
| **Soft Crack**        | **270–290°F / 132–143°C** | Flexible threads form             |
| **Hard Crack**        | **300–310°F / 149–154°C** | Brittle threads that snap         |

# Easy Rule to Remember

* **Boondi Ladoo → 1 string**
* **Kaju Katli → 1 string**
* **Coconut Burfi → 1 string to soft ball**
* **Peanut/Jaggery Ladoo → soft ball**

> **When in doubt, test early.** You can always cook syrup a little longer, but overcooked syrup is harder to correct.
