# Spicy or Salty Maida Diamonds — Air-Fried or Oil-Fried

Savory diamonds with salt and spices mixed directly into the dough. This version uses maida and milk for the dough and can be air-fried or deep-fried in oil.

**Salt is reduced from ⅓ tsp to ¼ tsp — 25% less added salt per batch.** Liquid oil replaces ghee in the dough. Air-frying gives a crisp, biscuit-like texture; frying in oil gives a more traditional fried texture.

These are suggested adaptations, not a kitchen-tested formula.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup maida/all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp neutral liquid oil, such as canola or groundnut oil
- ¼ tsp fine salt _this was too less, add to your taste_
- ½ tsp red chilli powder; increase to 1 tsp for more spice
- Pinch of baking powder
- pinch ajwain or cumin seeds( ajwain is acquired taste, kids may not like)
- ½ tsp sesame seeds, optional
- A pinch of turmeric, optional
- ¼ tsp crushed black pepper, optional
- Milk as needed; start with 3 tbsp and add by the teaspoon

**Plain salty version:** leave out chilli and turmeric. Keep ajwain or cumin for flavor; black pepper and sesame remain optional.

### Oil for cooking — choose one method

- **Air-frying:** optionally use up to 1 tsp additional neutral oil total for brushing all batches.
- **Frying in oil:** enough neutral oil for a depth of about 4–5 cm in a deep, heavy pan; keep the pan no more than half full.

## Steps

### 1. Prepare the dough

1. Mix the maida, salt, spices and optional seeds.
2. Rub in the tablespoon of oil thoroughly.
3. Add milk gradually and knead into a firm, pliable, nonsticky dough.
4. Cover and rest **20 minutes**. If rolling causes deep cracks, knead in a teaspoon of milk and reassess.

### 2. Roll and cut

1. Divide into two portions. Lightly dust the work surface and rolling pin with maida/all-purpose flour to prevent sticking.
2. Roll evenly to **2–3 mm thick**.
3. Cut parallel strips and then diagonal lines across them to make diamonds.
4. Prick lightly with a fork for flatter pieces and separate gently.

### 3. Cook — choose air-frying or frying in oil

#### Option A: Air-fry

1. Preheat according to your model's instructions to **180°C / 355°F**.
2. Brush off loose flour and arrange in a single layer with space between pieces. **Optional:** lightly brush both sides with up to 1 tsp additional oil total across all batches for more even browning.
3. Test a small batch first. **Check from 6 minutes; allow roughly 8–12 minutes**, turning halfway. These times are starting estimates for this thinner dough.
4. Remove when lightly golden; chilli makes the color darker. Cool a test piece and check that its center is crisp, without a soft doughy layer.
5. If needed, cook another 1–2 minutes at a time. Cool completely before storing airtight.

#### Option B: Fry in oil

1. Heat the frying oil over medium-low heat to about **160–170°C / 320–340°F**. A small dough scrap should rise with steady bubbles without browning immediately.
2. Brush off loose flour. Carefully add a small batch of diamonds without crowding the pan; no oil brushing is needed for this method.
3. Fry over medium-low heat, stirring and turning gently, until evenly light golden and crisp. Test a small batch first; thickness and oil temperature affect the time.
4. Lift out with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper or a wire rack. Let one piece cool and check that the center is dry and crisp; if it is doughy, lower the heat slightly and fry the next batch longer.
5. Let the oil return to temperature between batches. Cool the diamonds completely before storing airtight.

## Tips

- Use ajwain, cumin, pepper and chilli for flavor before increasing salt.
- Skip extra salt or chaat masala after cooking to retain the salt reduction.
- Avoid a wet dough, uneven thickness or an overcrowded basket.
- For air-frying, if the outside browns before the middle dries, lower the next batch's temperature by about 10°C and allow more time.
- Keep the tablespoon of oil in the dough; omitting it can make the diamonds tough.
- Serve a small portion in a bowl rather than eating directly from the storage jar.
