# Boondi Laddu 

## Ingredients

### Boondi

* 1¼ cups fine besan — about 160 g
* ¾ cup water — about 175–180 ml, added gradually
* Oil or ghee for frying

### Sugar Syrup

* 1¼ cups sugar — about 260 g
* ⅔ cup water — about 150 ml
* 3–4 cloves
* ½ tsp cardamom powder — optional

### For Shaping

* 1–2 tsp ghee

---

# 1. Make the Boondi Batter

1. Add besan to a bowl.
2. Add about **150 ml water** and whisk until smooth.
3. Rest for **15 minutes**.
4. Add more water, 1 tbsp at a time, until the batter flows easily through the boondi jhara.

Total water will usually be about **175–180 ml**.

### Batter Check

* **Round boondi:** correct consistency
* **Boondi with tails:** batter too thick → add a little water
* **Flat/joined boondi:** batter too thin → add a little besan

---

# 2. Fry the Boondi

1. Heat oil over medium to medium-high heat.
2. Hold the jhara about **3–4 inches above the oil**.
3. Pour batter onto the jhara and gently tap it through.
4. Fry only until the boondi is cooked.
5. Remove before it becomes crisp or dark.
6. Drain and repeat.

**Important:** The boondi should stay **soft**, so it can absorb the syrup.

---

# 3. Make the Sugar Syrup

Add to a wide pan:

* 260 g sugar
* 150 ml water
* Cloves

1. Heat over medium heat.
2. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
3. Let the syrup boil until it reaches **one-string consistency**.

### One-String Test

Cool a small drop for a few seconds.

Touch it between your thumb and finger and slowly separate them.

If **one thin string forms**, the syrup is ready.

Optional thermometer range:

**104–105°C / 220–222°F**

Immediately turn off the heat.

Add cardamom.

**Do not add extra water.**

---

# 4. Mix Boondi Into the Hot Syrup

1. Immediately add all the fried boondi to the hot syrup.
2. Gently mix for **30–60 seconds** until evenly coated.
3. Cover and rest for **5 minutes**.

Remember:

> **Hot syrup → add boondi**

---

# 5. Cool Until Warm

After 5 minutes:

1. Uncover and gently stir.
2. Let the mixture cool until it is **warm and comfortable to handle**.

If it is still too hot, transfer it to a wide steel tray or plate so it cools faster.

Do not let it become completely cold.

Remember:

> **Warm boondi → shape laddus**

---

# 6. Shape the Laddus

1. Lightly grease your palms with ghee.
2. Take a handful of the warm boondi mixture.
3. Squeeze firmly **3–4 times**.
4. Once it holds together, gently roll it into a ball.
5. Repeat.

The key is:

> **Squeeze first → then roll**

Let the finished laddus cool completely at room temperature.

---

# Quick Troubleshooting

### Mixture is too wet

Warm over **very low heat for 30–60 seconds**, mix, rest briefly, then shape.

### Mixture is dry or crystallizing

Add **1 tsp hot water**, mix gently, and test again.

### Mixture became too cold

Warm over very low heat for **20–30 seconds**, then shape while warm.

### Laddus still fall apart

Lightly crush about **½ cup boondi** and mix it back in. The smaller pieces help bind the laddus.

---

# Quick Cheat Sheet

**160 g besan + about 175–180 ml water**
→ Make flowing batter
→ Fry soft boondi

**260 g sugar + 150 ml water**
→ Cook to one-string syrup
→ Turn off heat

**Add boondi immediately**
→ Mix 30–60 sec
→ Cover 5 min
→ Cool until warm
→ Squeeze firmly
→ Roll into laddus

## Golden Rule

> **SOFT BOONDI → ONE-STRING SYRUP → HOT SYRUP FOR MIXING → WARM MIXTURE FOR SHAPING**
