# Quick Deepavali Spice–Jaggery Mix — No Water

Mix finely powdered jaggery, prepared spice powder, and melted ghee. **No fresh ginger, water, syrup, or cooking after mixing.** This is a simplified, untested variation with a grainy or crumbly texture, not the smooth texture of cooked lahiyam. Ghee binds the powders but does not dissolve jaggery.

**Mixing time:** about 5 minutes once the spices are prepared and cooled; roasting, grinding, and cooling take extra time.

## Ingredients

**Bold dry ingredients are required for this version; the others are optional.** Family recipes vary.

| Tamil name | English name | Quantity |
|---|---|---:|
| **Sukku (சுக்கு)** | **Dry ginger** | **15 g** |
| **Milagu (மிளகு)** | **Black pepper** | **5 g** |
| **Seeragam (சீரகம்)** | **Cumin** | **5 g** |
| **Dhaniya (தனியா)** | **Coriander seeds** | **8 g** |
| **Omam (ஓமம்)** | **Ajwain** | **3 g** |
| **Arisi thippili (அரிசி திப்பிலி)** | **Long pepper fruit** | **3 g** |
| **Kandathippili (கண்டத்திப்பிலி)** | **Long pepper stem** | **3 g** |
| **Chitharathai (சித்தரத்தை)** | **Lesser galangal** | **3 g** |
| Athimadhuram (அதிமதுரம்) | Licorice root | 2 g, optional; see note below |
| Elakkai (ஏலக்காய்) | Cardamom | 2 g, optional |
| Krambu (கிராம்பு) | Cloves | 1 g, optional |
| Pattai (பட்டை) | Cinnamon | 2 g, optional |

Also needed:

- **Fine, clean, ready-to-use jaggery powder:** start with the same weight as the finished, sieved spice powder (about 45–52 g before grinding losses); add more to taste
- **Melted ghee:** start with 1 tsp and add by ½ tsp until evenly moistened; the amount depends on your preferred texture

## Method

1. **Prepare the spices:** Break hard roots into small pieces. Gently dry-roast seeds and spices over very low heat until fragrant, without burning; warm hard roots separately. Cool completely, grind very finely, and sieve out coarse, woody pieces. If using ready-ground ingredients, gently warm the powders over very low heat, stirring briefly until fragrant, then cool; they scorch easily.
2. **Powder the jaggery:** Use fine, ready-to-use jaggery powder without visible grit or impurities. Break up lumps. Jaggery that needs dissolving and straining is unsuitable for this no-water method.
3. **Mix dry ingredients:** Weigh the sieved spice powder. Mix with an equal weight of powdered jaggery until evenly combined. This is a pungent starting ratio, not a tested final balance for this blend; adjust sweetness to taste.
4. **Add melted ghee:** Melt the ghee gently and let it become lukewarm. Stir in **1 tsp**, then add by **½ tsp** until the mixture is evenly moistened. Stop at a spoonable, crumbly mixture, or add a little more if you prefer it to hold together when pressed. Do not keep adding ghee to chase a smooth paste; jaggery will remain grainy.
5. **Taste and finish:** Taste a small amount. If too pungent, add powdered jaggery a little at a time and mix thoroughly. Transfer to a clean, dry jar. **Do not heat the combined mixture to melt the jaggery**—this method is intended to remain a dry mix bound with ghee.

## Storage and notes

- Refrigerate in a clean, dry airtight jar and use a dry spoon. Use within about 7 days as a conservative guideline; this variation has no tested shelf life. Ghee firms up when chilled, so the mixture may become firmer.
- This is a traditional-food-inspired mixture, not a proven treatment. For smooth cooked lahiyam, use the [traditional recipe](traditional_tamil_deepavali_marundhu.md).
- Licorice is optional; omit it if pregnant or if you have high blood pressure, heart or kidney disease, or interacting medications unless a clinician advises otherwise.
