# Quick Deepavali Marundhu — 5–7 Minute Version

For a tiny batch, use ready-ground spices and fine, ready-to-use jaggery powder.
This makes a simplified ginger-spice sweet paste with a less developed flavor
than the longer versions. Timing is an untested estimate; allow extra time if it
still tastes raw or remains runny. Cooling is extra.

### Ingredients

- 2 tbsp fine jaggery powder
- ¼ tsp sukku / dry ginger powder
- ¼ tsp cumin powder
- A small pinch of black pepper powder
- A small pinch of omam / ajwain powder, optional
- 1 tsp ghee
- 2 tsp water, plus a few drops if needed

### Method

1. **Measure — about 1 minute:** Have everything ready beside a small,
   heavy-bottomed pan.
2. **Dissolve — 1–2 minutes:** Heat jaggery and water over low heat, stirring
   until dissolved. If your jaggery needs straining, strain now and allow extra
   time. No string consistency is needed.
3. **Cook — 3–4 minutes:** Stir in the spice powders and ghee. Keep stirring
   over low heat until the paste is glossy, the raw spice aroma fades, and a
   spoon leaves a brief trail. Add a few drops of water if it starts sticking
   before the spices cook.
4. Turn off the heat while the paste is still soft; it thickens as it cools. Let
   cool before tasting. Serve a small culinary portion, around ½ tsp for an
   adult. Refrigerate the cooled paste promptly in a clean, dry airtight jar,
   use a dry spoon, and use within about 7 days as a conservative
   homemade-storage guideline.

**For this shortcut:** Skip fresh ginger, whole seeds, and woody roots. Do not
increase the heat to rush thickening—the small quantity can scorch quickly. This
is a culinary shortcut, not a medicinal equivalent of the traditional
preparation.
