#  Almond Ladoo (Badam Ladoo)

A simple badam ladoo made with superfine almond flour, powdered white sugar, ghee, and cardamom. No sugar syrup or milk needed.

- **Preparation and cooking time:** About 15–20 minutes
- **Yield:** Approximately 10–12 small ladoos

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Superfine blanched almond flour | 100 g |
| White sugar, finely powdered and sifted | 60 g |
| Ghee | 35–40 g total |
| Cardamom powder | ¼ tsp |

## Instructions

1. **Powder the sugar.** Grind regular white sugar to a very fine powder. Sift to remove any coarse granules. If using ready-made powdered sugar, check whether it contains added starch.
2. **Gently roast the almond flour.** Heat a heavy-bottomed pan over the lowest heat. Add the almond flour and stir continuously for 2–3 minutes, just until fragrant. Keep it pale; do not brown it.
3. **Add ghee.** Add about 30 g of ghee and stir for approximately 1 minute. Turn off the heat.
4. **Cool until warm.** Let the almond mixture cool until comfortably warm to the touch, not hot.
5. **Add sugar and cardamom.** Mix in the sifted powdered sugar and cardamom thoroughly for 1–2 minutes, until evenly distributed.
6. **Rest briefly.** Let the mixture sit for 2–3 minutes so the ghee distributes evenly.
7. **Shape the ladoos.** Take small portions and press firmly into smooth balls. If the mixture crumbles, add the remaining ghee a little at a time, mixing and testing until it holds together. Avoid adding too much ghee at once.

## Secrets to a Soft, Melt-in-the-Mouth Texture

- **Choose superfine blanched almond flour.** Coarse almond meal gives a grainier texture.
- **Use finely powdered sugar.** Sugar does not completely dissolve in the almond flour; a fine grind prevents noticeable grit.
- **Roast gently.** Over-roasting can darken the flour and change the delicate flavor.
- **Add sugar only after cooling slightly.** Adding it to a very hot mixture can cause partial melting and an uneven texture.
- **Use enough ghee to bind.** Start with 30 g and gradually increase toward 35–40 g only if needed.
- **Avoid water or milk.** Neither is necessary for binding, and both can shorten shelf life.

## Should You Dissolve the Sugar First?

**No.** For this dry-style ladoo, finely powdered sugar blends with the warm almond flour and ghee. It will not completely dissolve, but the finished ladoos should feel smooth. Making sugar syrup is a different technique and can produce a firmer or chewier result if overcooked.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place for about 4–5 days, or refrigerate for up to 2 weeks. Use clean, dry utensils and discard if the aroma or appearance changes.
