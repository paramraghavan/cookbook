# Indian Air-Fryer Recipes and Cooking Guide

A practical starter collection of vegetarian snacks and dry vegetable dishes, with ingredients, steps and a quick settings chart. Recipes are summarized and adapted from the linked authors; this collection has not been kitchen-tested as a whole.

## How to use this guide

1. Select **Air Fry** mode. Follow your model's instructions for preheating and accessories.
2. Prepare and dry ingredients before adding oil and spices.
3. Arrange food in a single layer with room for air to circulate. Cook additional batches rather than piling food up.
4. Shake loose foods or turn larger pieces halfway through, unless a recipe says otherwise.
5. Check at the earliest listed time, then add 1–2 minutes at a time as needed. Basket size, food thickness and model affect cooking time.
6. Check the inside as well as the color. Let crunchy snacks cool briefly before judging crispness.

**Temperatures below include both units.** 350°F is about 175°C, not 350°C. Choose the nearest available setting; the Celsius conversions are rounded. Cooking times exclude preheating, preparation and extra batches.

Use only accessories approved for your model. Keep loose parchment away from the heating element and never preheat with an empty paper liner. A fine-mesh insert approved for your fryer is useful when nuts would fall through the basket.

## 1. Whole Roasted Peanuts

### Ingredients

- 1 cup raw, shelled peanuts
- Salt, optional

### Steps

1. Preheat to **160°C / 320°F**.
2. Spread the peanuts in a single layer; use a suitable insert if they would fall through.
3. Cook **6–8 minutes**, shaking at least twice. Start checking early to avoid scorching.
4. Spread on a plate and cool completely. Peanuts become crisper as they cool.
5. Rub off the skins if desired and season. **Keep the peanuts whole** for snacking or your peanut ladoo recipe.

Source: [Indian Veggie Delight — roasted peanuts](https://www.indianveggiedelight.com/air-fryer-roasted-peanuts/).

## 2. Besan-Coated Masala Cashews

### Ingredients

- 1 cup raw cashews
- 1 tsp oil
- ½ cup besan
- 1 tbsp rice flour
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1½ tsp mild Kashmiri chilli powder
- ⅛ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chaat masala
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp salt, or less if the chaat masala is salty
- About 2 tbsp water, added gradually

### Steps

1. Toss cashews with oil. Combine the flours, paste and seasonings separately, then mix with the nuts.
2. Add only enough water for the coating to cling. Avoid a runny batter.
3. Preheat to **165°C / 325°F**. Spread the nuts in one layer.
4. Cook about **10 minutes**, shaking halfway. Check for a dry coating without raw besan flavor.
5. Cool fully before judging crunch or storing airtight.

Source: [Cook With Manali — masala cashews](https://www.cookwithmanali.com/air-fryer-masala-cashews/).

## 3. Onion Pakoda

### Ingredients

- 1 large onion, about 250 g, thinly sliced
- ½ cup besan
- 1 tbsp rice flour
- 3 tbsp chopped coriander leaves
- 1–2 chopped green chillies, optional
- ¼ tsp each ajwain, turmeric and red chilli powder
- ½ tsp coriander powder
- A pinch of garam masala
- ½–¾ tsp salt, to taste
- 1 tsp oil, plus a little for brushing
- Water as needed, approximately 3–5 tbsp

### Steps

1. Massage salt into the separated onion slices; rest **10 minutes**.
2. Optionally dry-roast besan until fragrant, then cool slightly.
3. Add flours, spices, coriander and oil to the onions. Add water gradually to make a thick, clinging mixture.
4. Preheat and lightly grease the cooking surface. Arrange small, slightly flattened clusters with gaps.
5. Cook at **170°C / 340°F for 10 minutes**.
6. Brush lightly with oil, then cook at **200°C / 400°F for 4–5 minutes**, until browned and cooked inside. Serve hot.

Source: [Cook With Manali — onion pakoda](https://www.cookwithmanali.com/air-fryer-onion-pakoda/).

## 4. Savory Maida Diamonds / Namak Para

This is an air-fryer dough adapted from the source below; its ghee and sooji proportions differ from the deep-fried diamond recipe.

### Ingredients

- 1 cup maida
- 2 tbsp sooji
- 1½ tbsp melted ghee
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp ajwain
- Approximately ¼ cup water, added gradually
- A little oil for brushing
- Optional adaptation: ¼–½ tsp chilli powder for spicy diamonds

### Steps

1. Mix maida, sooji, salt and ajwain. Rub in ghee thoroughly.
2. Add water gradually and make a firm dough. Cover and rest **20–30 minutes**.
3. Roll about **5–6 mm thick** and cut into diamonds. Thinner pieces will cook faster.
4. Preheat, arrange in one layer and brush lightly with oil.
5. Cook at **190°C / 370°F for 10–12 minutes**, checking from 8 minutes and turning halfway.
6. Cool a piece and check its center is crisp. Cool the batch completely before storing.

For plain salty diamonds, leave out chilli. Expect a crisper, less flaky texture than deep-fried diamonds.

Source: [Cook With Manali — namak para, including air-fryer method](https://www.cookwithmanali.com/namak-pare/).

## 5. Paneer Tikka

### Ingredients — small batch

- 225 g paneer, cut into large cubes
- ½ cup onion chunks, layers separated
- ½ cup capsicum chunks
- ¼ cup thick plain yogurt
- 1 tsp each grated ginger and minced garlic
- 1½ tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp each garam masala and coriander powder
- ½ tsp salt, to taste
- ¾ tsp crushed kasuri methi
- A little oil for brushing

### Steps

1. Combine yogurt, ginger, garlic, lemon and seasonings.
2. Gently coat paneer and vegetables. Cover and refrigerate at least **30 minutes**.
3. Preheat and grease the basket. Arrange coated pieces in one layer; skewers are optional.
4. Brush lightly with oil. Cook at **180°C / 360°F for 5 minutes**.
5. Turn and cook approximately **5 minutes more**. Stop when edges brown and vegetables soften; prolonged cooking toughens paneer.
6. Serve hot with chutney and lemon.

Adapted, approximately half-sized: [Piping Pot Curry — paneer tikka](https://pipingpotcurry.com/paneer-tikka/).

## 6. Aloo Gobi

### Ingredients

- 4 cups cauliflower florets, washed and dried
- 1 cup peeled potato cubes, about 1 cm across
- ½ cup sliced onion
- ½ cup chopped tomato
- 2 tbsp oil
- ½ tsp each garlic powder, cumin powder, turmeric and Kashmiri chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ¾–1 tsp salt, to taste
- 1 tsp lemon or lime juice
- Chopped coriander leaves

### Steps

1. Cut potatoes smaller than the cauliflower so they finish cooking together.
2. Toss vegetables with oil, salt and spices.
3. Preheat and spread a single layer in the basket; this quantity may need several batches.
4. Cook at **195°C / 380°F for 12–15 minutes**, shaking halfway.
5. Pierce a potato cube. If it is firm inside, remove cooked cauliflower and continue the potatoes in short intervals.
6. Finish with lemon and coriander. Serve with roti or rice.

Source: [Piping Pot Curry — roasted aloo gobi](https://pipingpotcurry.com/roasted-aloo-gobi/).

## 7. Kurkuri Bhindi

### Ingredients

- 300 g bhindi, washed and thoroughly dried
- 4 tbsp besan
- 2 tbsp rice flour
- 1 tsp chilli powder, or less to taste
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½–¾ tsp salt
- 2 tsp oil
- 1–2 tbsp water, only as needed for the coating to cling
- Optional: chaat masala or amchoor to finish

### Steps

1. Trim bhindi and cut lengthwise into quarters. Keep the knife and board dry.
2. Mix with flours, spices and oil. Add water sparingly: the coating should cling rather than drip.
3. Preheat to **180°C / 355°F**. Arrange loosely in one layer.
4. Cook **10 minutes**, turning halfway. Check, then continue in short intervals until crisp; some batches may need **20–25 minutes total**.
5. Rest uncovered for 2 minutes, add finishing seasoning and serve promptly.

Source: [Cook With Renu — kurkuri bhindi](https://cookwithrenu.com/air-fryer-kurkuri-bhindi/), using the quantities in its recipe card. The article gives differing quantities and timings elsewhere; check doneness rather than relying on a fixed finish time.

## 8–10. Simple Uses From Asha Sharma's Video

These are brief adaptations of the video you shared, not independently tested recipes. Fahrenheit readings are converted below.

### 8. Refresh softened almonds

**Ingredients:** 1 cup almonds that have softened but are still fresh, with no rancid smell or mold.

1. Spread in one layer.
2. Cook at **150°C / 300°F**, checking after 5 minutes; the video suggests 10 minutes total.
3. Cool completely before checking crunch or storing. Reheating does not rescue spoiled nuts.

[Video section: nuts, 4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cOyBpvVofUM&t=296s).

### 9. Tomatoes for curry or chutney

**Ingredients:** 2–3 whole tomatoes.

1. Score a shallow cross through each skin.
2. Cook at **175°C / 350°F for about 6 minutes**, until the skin splits.
3. Cool enough to handle, peel, then use in your stovetop curry or chutney.

[Video section: tomatoes, 5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cOyBpvVofUM&t=333s).

### 10. Corn with lemon and chilli

**Ingredients:** 2 ears of corn in their husks; lemon, salt and chilli powder to finish.

1. If your model permits cooking with husks, rinse the ears, trim loose silk and fit them clear of the element.
2. Cook at **175°C / 350°F for about 20 minutes**, turning halfway.
3. Check tenderness. Cool slightly, remove husks and silk, then rub with lemon, salt and chilli.

[Video section: corn, 6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cOyBpvVofUM&t=382s).

## More Ideas to Try Next

These are recipe ideas and discovery links, not additional tested cooking schedules in this guide.

- **Aloo tikki / vegetable cutlets:** cooked potato and vegetable patties.
- **Sabudana vada:** soaked sabudana, potato and peanuts formed into patties.
- **Samosa:** shaped potato-filled pastry, brushed with oil.
- **Tandoori gobi or mushrooms:** thick yogurt-and-spice coating.
- **Chickpea snacks:** cooked chickpeas, thoroughly drained and dried before roasting.
- **Paneer puffs:** paneer filling enclosed in puff pastry.
- **Gujiya:** filled sweet pastry; sealing and cooking depend on filling and size.
