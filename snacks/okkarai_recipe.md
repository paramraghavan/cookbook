# Traditional Okkarai

A classic Deepavali sweet made with chana dal, jaggery, coconut, ghee and
cardamom. **Yield:** 6 servings · **Time:** 45–60 minutes.

> **Texture goal:** Soft, moist, separate crumbs—not a smooth halwa.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                  | Amount                        |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Chana dal (kadalai paruppu) | 1 cup (200 g)                 |
| Jaggery, grated             | 150–200 g (175 g recommended) |
| Fresh grated coconut        | ¼ cup (25 g)                  |
| Ghee                        | 3–4 tbsp (about 45 g)         |
| Cardamom powder             | ½ tsp                         |
| Cashews                     | 10–12, broken                 |
| Water for cooking dal       | About 2½ cups                 |
| Water for jaggery syrup     | ¼ cup                         |
| Salt                        | Tiny pinch, optional          |

## Instructions

### 1. Dry-roast the chana dal

Heat a heavy kadai over medium-low heat. Add the dry chana dal and stir for *
*4–6 minutes**, until fragrant and lightly golden. Do not darken it too much.

### 2. Pressure-cook and drain

Wash the roasted dal. Cook with approximately **2½ cups water** until tender:
about **3–4 whistles** in a stovetop pressure cooker, or **10–12 minutes at high
pressure** in an Instant Pot with natural release. Cooking times vary by cooker
and dal age. Drain thoroughly; the dal should mash easily but should not be
watery.

### 3. Mash coarsely

Cool briefly. Mash with a potato masher, or pulse a few times in a mixer. **Do
not puree:** retain small, distinct dal granules.

### 4. Prepare jaggery syrup

Warm the jaggery with **¼ cup water** until dissolved. Strain out impurities.
Return to the saucepan and simmer just until slightly sticky; **no string
consistency is needed**.

### 5. Combine and cook

Heat **1 tbsp of the measured ghee** in a wide, heavy kadai. Add the mashed dal
and strained jaggery syrup; mix well. Stir in coconut. Cook over medium-low
heat, stirring frequently, until excess moisture evaporates and the mixture
begins to separate into soft crumbs.

### 6. Finish with ghee, cashews and cardamom

Add the remaining ghee gradually while stirring. In a small pan, fry cashews in
a little of that measured ghee until golden. Fold them into the Ukkarai with the
cardamom powder and optional pinch of salt. Remove from heat when the mixture is
**crumbly but still slightly moist**; it firms as it cools.

## Troubleshooting

- **Too sticky:** Cook uncovered a little longer over low heat, stirring
  continuously.
- **Too dry:** Fold in 1–2 tsp warm ghee; avoid prolonged cooking.
- **Too smooth:** Next time, drain better and pulse the cooked dal less.
- **Too sweet:** Use 150 g jaggery next time; 175 g is a balanced starting
  point.

## Storage

Cool promptly and refrigerate in a sealed container for **3–4 days**, especially
when made with fresh coconut. Reheat gently if desired.
