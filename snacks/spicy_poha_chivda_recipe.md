# Best Crispy & Spicy Poha Chivda (Aval Mixture)

A crunchy, spicy South Indian-style snack made with thin flattened rice (aval/poha), roasted peanuts, cashews, roasted gram, curry leaves, and aromatic spices.

This recipe is **garlic-free**, uses minimal oil, and is perfect as an evening snack with masala chai.

## Recipe Information

- **Preparation Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cooking Time:** 15–20 minutes
- **Total Time:** 20–25 minutes
- **Servings:** 3–4
- **Spice Level:** Medium to spicy
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Cooking Method:** Dry-roasting and tempering
- **Diet:** Vegetarian, garlic-free

---

## 1. Ingredients

### Main Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Thin poha (thin aval) | 3 cups |
| Raw peanuts | ½ cup |
| Roasted gram (pottukadalai) | ¼ cup |
| Cashews | ¼ cup |
| Oil | 2–3 tbsp |

### Tempering Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Mustard seeds | ½ tsp |
| Curry leaves | 2 sprigs |
| Dried red chilies | 3–4 |
| Green chilies, slit | 1–2 |
| Hing (asafoetida) | ⅛ tsp |

### Spice Powders and Seasoning

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Turmeric powder | ½ tsp |
| Kashmiri red chili powder | 1 tsp |
| Hot red chili powder | ¼–½ tsp |
| Salt | ¾ tsp, adjust to taste |
| Sugar | ½ tsp (optional) |
| Amchur powder | ½ tsp (optional) |

---

## 2. Preparation

### Step 1 — Dry-Roast the Poha

1. Heat a wide, heavy-bottomed kadai over **low heat**.
2. Add 3 cups of thin poha without any oil.
3. Gently stir and roast for approximately 4–6 minutes.
4. Continue until the poha becomes light and crispy.
5. Transfer the roasted poha to a large bowl and set aside.

**Important:**
- Do not wash or soak the poha.
- Use paper-thin poha for the best results.
- Keep the heat low to prevent browning.
- The poha should break easily when pressed between your fingers.

### Step 2 — Roast the Peanuts, Cashews, and Roasted Gram

1. Add 2–3 tbsp oil to the same kadai.
2. Heat the oil over **medium-low heat**.
3. Add ½ cup raw peanuts.
4. Stir frequently and roast for approximately 4–6 minutes until crunchy.
5. Remove the peanuts using a slotted spoon and set aside.
6. Add ¼ cup cashews to the remaining oil.
7. Fry until lightly golden, then remove and set aside.
8. Add ¼ cup roasted gram (pottukadalai).
9. Stir for approximately 30 seconds to warm and lightly crisp it.
10. Remove and set aside.

**Important:**

Remove all the roasted nuts and roasted gram before preparing the tempering.

This prevents them from burning while the mustard seeds, chilies, and curry leaves are being cooked.

### Step 3 — Prepare the Tempering (Tadka)

Use the same kadai with the remaining oil.

#### A. Splutter the Mustard Seeds

1. Heat the remaining oil over **medium-low heat**.
2. Add ½ tsp mustard seeds.
3. Allow the mustard seeds to heat until they begin **popping and spluttering**.
4. This usually takes approximately 15–30 seconds, depending on the oil temperature.
5. Once the spluttering slows down, reduce the heat to low.

**Important:**

Do not add the curry leaves or chilies before the mustard seeds begin spluttering.

Mustard seeds release their characteristic flavor when they pop in hot oil.

#### B. Add Chilies and Curry Leaves

1. Add 3–4 dried red chilies, broken into pieces.
2. Add 1–2 slit green chilies.
3. Stir briefly for approximately 10–15 seconds.
4. Carefully add 2 sprigs of clean, thoroughly dried curry leaves.
5. Fry until the curry leaves become crispy and the sizzling subsides.
6. Add ⅛ tsp hing and stir briefly.

**Important:**
- Curry leaves must be dry to minimize oil splattering.
- Keep the heat low enough to prevent the chilies from burning.
- Crispy curry leaves provide excellent aroma and flavor.

### Step 4 — Add the Spice Powders

1. Reduce the heat to its lowest setting.
2. Add:
   - ½ tsp turmeric powder
   - 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
   - ¼–½ tsp hot red chili powder
   - ¾ tsp salt
   - ½ tsp sugar (optional)
3. Stir quickly for approximately 5–10 seconds.

**Important:**

Spice powders can burn quickly in hot oil.

If the kadai is too hot, switch off the heat temporarily before adding the spice powders.

The spices should become aromatic without turning dark or bitter.

### Step 5 — Combine Everything

1. Keep the heat on low.
2. Return the roasted peanuts to the kadai.
3. Add the roasted cashews.
4. Add the roasted gram.
5. Mix gently until the nuts are evenly coated with the spices.
6. Add the dry-roasted poha.
7. Gently fold everything together.
8. Continue mixing over low heat for approximately 1–2 minutes.
9. Make sure the turmeric and chili powders coat the poha evenly.
10. Turn off the heat.

### Step 6 — Finish with Amchur

1. Sprinkle ½ tsp amchur powder over the chivda.
2. Mix gently to distribute the tangy flavor.
3. Taste and adjust salt or chili powder if necessary.
4. Allow the chivda to cool completely.

**Tip:** Add amchur after turning off the heat to preserve its tangy flavor.

## 3. Secrets to Perfect Crispy Chivda

### 1. Use Thin Poha

Paper-thin poha is ideal because it becomes crispy quickly without deep-frying.

Thick poha requires a different cooking method.

### 2. Roast Ingredients Separately

Roast the poha, peanuts, cashews, and roasted gram separately.

This ensures that each ingredient becomes crispy without burning.

### 3. Splutter Mustard Seeds Properly

Always allow mustard seeds to pop in hot oil before adding other tempering ingredients.

This develops their characteristic aroma.

### 4. Make Curry Leaves Crispy

Crispy curry leaves give the chivda its distinctive South Indian flavor.

Use thoroughly dried curry leaves to reduce splattering.

### 5. Use Two Types of Chili Powder

- **Kashmiri chili powder:** Provides vibrant red color and mild heat.
- **Hot red chili powder:** Adds the desired spiciness.

### 6. Balance Spicy, Salty, and Tangy Flavors

A small amount of sugar balances the heat.

Amchur adds a pleasant tanginess.

### 7. Avoid Burning the Spices

Add powdered spices only over very low heat or with the heat temporarily switched off.

### 8. Cool Completely Before Storing

Do not close the storage container while the chivda is warm.

Trapped moisture can make it lose its crispiness.

## 4. Spice Level Adjustments

Quantities below are for 3 cups of thin poha.

| Spice Level | Kashmiri Chili Powder | Hot Chili Powder | Green Chilies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mild | ½ tsp | None | 0–1 |
| Medium | 1 tsp | ¼ tsp | 1 |
| Hot | 1 tsp | ½ tsp | 2 |
| Extra Hot | 1½ tsp | 1 tsp | 2–3 |

**Recommended:** Use the Hot combination for a flavorful South Indian-style spicy chivda.

---

## 5. Optional Variations

### Extra Crunchy Chivda

Add:
- 2 tbsp additional roasted peanuts
- 2 tbsp roasted cashews
- 2 tbsp roasted coconut slices

### Extra Spicy Chivda

Add:
- ½ tsp additional hot chili powder
- ¼ tsp black pepper powder
- 1 additional dried red chili

### Tangy Chivda

Add:
- ½ tsp additional amchur powder

Alternatively, sprinkle a little chaat masala after turning off the heat.

### Low-Oil Chivda

- Dry-roast the poha.
- Dry-roast the peanuts and cashews separately.
- Use approximately 1–2 tsp oil for tempering.
- Mix everything together over low heat.

---

## 6. Storage Instructions

1. Allow the chivda to cool completely.
2. Transfer it to a clean, dry, airtight container.
3. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
4. For best quality, consume within approximately 1–2 weeks.
5. Always use a clean, dry spoon.

**Tip:** If the chivda loses some crispiness, gently dry-roast it over low heat for a short time and allow it to cool before serving.

---

## 7. Quick Cooking Sequence

For easy reference while cooking:

1. **Dry-roast poha** → Remove.
2. **Roast peanuts** → Remove.
3. **Roast cashews** → Remove.
4. **Lightly roast pottukadalai** → Remove.
5. **Heat remaining oil.**
6. **Add mustard seeds** → Wait until they splutter.
7. **Add dried red chilies and green chilies.**
8. **Add dry curry leaves** → Fry until crispy.
9. **Add hing.**
10. **Reduce heat and add spice powders.**
11. **Return peanuts, cashews, and roasted gram.**
12. **Add roasted poha.**
13. **Mix gently for 1–2 minutes.**
14. **Turn off heat and add amchur.**
15. **Cool completely and store.**

## 8. Final Recommended Flavor Combination

For 3 cups of thin poha:

- ½ cup peanuts
- ¼ cup cashews
- ¼ cup roasted gram
- 2 sprigs curry leaves
- ½ tsp mustard seeds
- 3–4 dried red chilies
- 2 green chilies
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder
- ½ tsp hot chili powder
- ½ tsp amchur powder
- ½ tsp sugar (optional)
- Salt to taste

**Result:** Crispy, spicy, aromatic, garlic-free South Indian aval mixture with crunchy peanuts and curry leaves.

**Best served with:** Hot masala chai or South Indian filter coffee.
