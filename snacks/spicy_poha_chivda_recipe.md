# Spicy Poha Chivda (Aval Mixture) with Peanuts

A crispy, spicy South Indian-style snack made with thin flattened rice (aval/poha), peanuts, curry leaves, and spices.

- **Preparation and cooking:** 15–20 minutes
- **Yield:** Approximately 2–3 cups
- **Difficulty:** Easy

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Thin poha / thin aval | 2 cups |
| Raw peanuts | ½ cup |
| Roasted gram (pottukadalai) | ¼ cup (optional) |
| Curry leaves | 1 sprig |
| Oil | 2 tbsp |
| Mustard seeds | ½ tsp |
| Turmeric powder | ¼ tsp |
| Red chili powder | ½–1 tsp |
| Green chilies | 1–2, slit (optional) |
| Hing (asafoetida) | A pinch |
| Salt | To taste |
| Sugar | ¼ tsp (optional) |
| Amchur powder | ¼ tsp (optional) |

## Instructions

### 1. Roast the poha

1. Heat a wide kadai or frying pan over low heat.
2. Add the thin poha without oil.
3. Roast gently for 3–5 minutes, stirring, until crisp.
4. Transfer to a bowl and set aside.

**Important:** Do not wash or soak the poha.

### 2. Roast the peanuts

1. Add the oil to the kadai.
2. Fry the peanuts over medium-low heat for approximately 4–6 minutes, until golden and crunchy.
3. Add roasted gram, if using, and stir briefly.

### 3. Prepare the seasoning

1. Reduce heat to low.
2. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
3. Add curry leaves, green chilies, and hing.
4. Fry until the curry leaves are crisp and moisture from the chilies has evaporated.
5. Add turmeric and red chili powder; stir briefly without burning the spices.

### 4. Combine

1. Add the roasted poha to the kadai.
2. Add salt, optional sugar, and optional amchur powder.
3. Toss gently over very low heat for 1–2 minutes, until evenly coated.
4. Turn off the heat and allow the chivda to cool completely.

## Variations and tips

- **Extra spicy:** Add another ½ tsp red chili powder or a pinch of black pepper.
- **South Indian flavor:** Add crushed dried red chilies and extra curry leaves.
- **Extra crunch:** Include roasted cashews, coconut slices, or additional peanuts.
- **Lower oil:** Dry-roast the poha and peanuts separately, then use about 1 tsp oil for tempering.
- **Storage:** Once completely cool, store in a dry airtight container. It can stay crispy for approximately 1–2 weeks when stored properly.

## Choosing the right poha

Use **thin poha (paper-thin aval)**. Thick poha generally requires a different roasting or frying method to become crispy.
