 Tamil Iyer-Style Green Mango Pickle (மாங்காய் ஊறுகாய்)

A simple traditional Tamil Iyer-style green mango pickle made with
mustard, red chilli, turmeric, sesame oil, salt, fenugreek, and
asafoetida. This version contains no garlic or onion.

Ingredients

For about 500 g raw green mango:

• Raw sour green mango – 500 g
• Salt – 2½–3 tbsp
• Red chilli powder – 2–3 tbsp
• Turmeric powder – ½ tsp
• Mustard seeds – 2 tsp
• Fenugreek seeds (vendhayam) – ½ tsp
• Asafoetida (perungayam) – ¼–½ tsp
• Gingelly/sesame oil (nallennai) – 4–5 tbsp

Method

1. Prepare the mango

Wash the mangoes and dry them completely. Cut them into small
pieces, leaving the skin on.

> **Important:** There should be no water on the mangoes, utensils, or
> storage jar.

2. Prepare the vendhaya powder

Dry-roast the fenugreek seeds on low heat until aromatic. Cool
completely and grind to a fine or slightly coarse powder.

Do not over-roast the fenugreek, as it can become bitter.

3. Mix the mango

Place the mango pieces in a dry bowl.

Add:

• Salt
• Red chilli powder
• Turmeric powder
• Roasted fenugreek powder

Mix thoroughly so that all the mango pieces are coated with the spices.

4. Prepare the tempering

Heat the sesame oil.

Add the mustard seeds and allow them to splutter.

Turn off the heat and add the asafoetida.

Allow the seasoned oil to cool until warm.

5. Combine

Pour the seasoned oil over the mango and spice mixture.

Mix thoroughly.

6. Rest the pickle

Transfer the pickle to a clean, completely dry glass jar.

Leave it for 1–2 days, stirring once or twice with a clean, dry
spoon.

The mango will gradually release some juice, which combines with the
spices and oil to form the pickle gravy.

Tamil Iyer-Style Notes

For this style of mango pickle:

• Use nallennai (gingelly/sesame oil) rather than mustard oil.
• Use mustard seeds, vendhayam, and perungayam for the
characteristic Tamil pickle flavor.
• Skip fennel seeds.
• No onion or garlic is needed.

Serving Suggestions

Serve with:

• Thayir sadam (curd rice)
• Sambar sadam
• Rasam sadam
• Poricha kuzhambu
• Molagootal
• Other traditional South Indian meals
